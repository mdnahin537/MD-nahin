## 1.2 What even the A+ work got wrong or missed

**M1 — NEW CRITICAL: the auto-backup writes the user's API key to disk in plaintext.** The file has a dedicated scrubber, `_stateForPersist()` (L6604–6613), whose own comment says it is "Used by **every** persistence sink" (L6601–6603) **[read]**. Persist (L6731), persistNow (L6752), snapshots (L7016), and export (L8851) all scrub the key **[read]**. But `AutoSave._write` serializes **raw `State.data`** (`JSON.stringify(State.data,null,2)`, L6302) and so does `_fallbackDownload` (L6318) **[read]** — and the runtime key is deliberately hydrated into `State.data.meta.settings.copilotKey` at load (L6638, L6666) **[read]**. So every licensed user with auto-backup configured has their OpenRouter key sitting in `realmwright-backup.json` — the exact file users move between machines, sync to cloud drives, and share ("here's my realm"). This violates the file's own P1.13 standard and CLAUDE.md's hard constraint ("never expose credentials"). POSITION §10 has no row for it; POSITION §5 praises the export path's key hygiene while the *other* disk path leaks. Fix is one line per site: serialize `_stateForPersist()` instead. **This must join the criticals.**

**M2 — NEW CRITICAL-CLASS: the "own your data" product stores the world in storage the browser is allowed to delete.** Grep `navigator.storage` / `storage.persist` → **zero hits** **[read]**. The entire world — and the snapshot system meant to protect it — lives in best-effort IndexedDB plus a localStorage mirror (L6737–6746) **[read]**. Browsers may evict best-effort storage under disk pressure without asking; the app never requests durable storage (`navigator.storage.persist()`), never checks `persisted()`, and never tells the user their world's storage class. The only true on-disk path is AutoSave — which is (a) **license-gated** (L6261: "only auto-save for licensed users") **[read]**, (b) **Chromium-only** (`'showSaveFilePicker' in window`, L6254) **[read]**, and (c) for licensed users on Firefox/Safari degrades to an unsolicited file *download every 20 actions* (L6264→L6267→L6316–6324) **[read]** — spam or silently blocked, never designed. Net: a demo user, an unlicensed user, or any Firefox/Safari user has **zero automatic durability**; their safety net is remembering to click Export. POSITION's data-loss criticals (the 400 ms debounce window) name the small hole and miss the big one. The fix set is small (request persistent storage at first realm-write; surface backup status; un-gate AutoSave — see Ruling 5) but the *claim* "your world is yours, forever" is currently architecture-unbacked.

# 2. THE CEO VERDICT

## 2.1 The single most important strategic decision

**Make the two claims true before making them loud — trust first, autonomy second, in that order, before any public word.** Concretely: (1) the **Trust Package** (the four POSITION criticals + M1 key-scrub + M2 durable storage + honor-ware licensing) makes "own your data" architecturally true; (2) the **World Turn** makes "the world moves without you" true. Everything else — pricing, identity copy, demo, distribution — is sequencing around those two. The staff had the second half of this (IDEAS §1.0); they missed that the first half was equally unbuilt (M1/M2/M3). A product whose pitch is *trust + a living world* currently leaks credentials into backups, lets browsers evict worlds, gates safety behind a self-revoking license, and has a world that holds perfectly still. Close those four gaps and the pitch is not marketing — it is description.

## 2.2 What actually wins this market

Not features. The category is crowded with hosted AI tools (R-Q1) and saturated with free storage (R-Q2). What wins is **a demonstrated feeling plus a trustable structure**:

1. **The 90-second proof.** A visitor opens the free demo, sees a coiled world (Heartbeat), clicks once, gets a usable prep sheet (Tonight-Lite), ends a fake session, and **watches the world take its turn**. No key, no signup, no server cost (deterministic = $0 marginal). Every rival's demo needs their cloud AI; ours runs on math. That asymmetry is the whole go-to-market.
2. **Ownership that survives audit.** The buyer vocabulary is literally "own your data / no lock-in / works offline / buy once" (R-Q4). After the Trust Package, we are the only product in the category where that is verifiable: file on your disk, world in durable storage, backup you can read, export that loses nothing (L8846 **[read]**), key that never leaves your machine.
3. **State, not generation.** Against hosted AI rivals *and* against free chatbots (M6a), the defensible AI story is that the AI here operates on a persistent, deterministic, owned world-state it can read and carefully write (COPILOT_TOOLS L9346, gated writes P-§4). Generation is a commodity; a world that *remembers and moves* is not.
4. **Price honesty as a weapon** (R-T3): one-time, BYO-key, demo before the number. Old Greg's proves the structural trap we escape (hosted AI ate their one-time model); BYO-key means our marginal cost per user is ~$0 forever.

## 2.3 The biggest existential risk

**Obscurity, not competition.** The entire A+ chain produced excellent product truth and zero acquisition plan. A solo founder in Dhaka with no audience, selling a single HTML file via Gumroad, fails by default at distribution: nobody arrives. R-Q8 names channels (itch browse, EN World, r/DMAcademy, Sly Flourish's lazy-DM audience, GM YouTube) but no artifact owns the question "where do the first 5,000 visitors come from?" My direction (§5): the **free deterministic demo on itch is the distribution engine**, the **shareable World-Turn report is the viral artifact** (a GM posting "look what my world did between sessions" is organic reach with attribution baked in), and a **weekly build-in-public/lazy-DM content beat** is the founder-time tax that must be budgeted like engineering. Secondary existential risk: a **trust-betrayal event at small scale** — one Reddit post titled "RealmWright put my API key in a backup file" or "my world vanished" kills an ownership-positioned product permanently. That is why M1/M2 are criticals, not polish. Tertiary: **founder bandwidth** — Hunter runs four ventures; the roadmap must survive on small, completable phases (it does — each phase below is weeks-scale agent work, not months).

## 2.5 What we are fooling ourselves about

1. **"$49-grade without AI" — today it is not.** IDEAS §1.a already conceded ~$15–19-grade against free Obsidian; I co-sign after walking the code. The floor becomes real only when the World Turn, Tonight-Lite, and House-Rules ship. Until then the no-AI half is a beautifully-written *reactive* toy plus a great export.
2. **"Offline, yours forever" — currently evictable** (M2). Architecture must catch up to copy.
3. **"The license protects revenue" — it protects nothing** (M3) and the file is trivially copyable anyway. Piracy of a $19–29 single file is a marketing channel wearing a disguise; goodwill + updates + low price is the only rational response. Stop pretending otherwise and bank the trust instead.
4. **"Our AI features differentiate" — raw generation is a commodity** the user's own chatbot does free (M6a). Only state + integration differentiate.
5. **"Solo mode is clean" — it ships a competitor's published table under framing the file's own standard forbids** (verified, 1.1). It is the one surface that could embarrass us on both halal and IP grounds in the same forum post.

## 2.6 Go / adjust / no-go

**GO, with four adjustments.** The direction (dual-mode, one-time, BYO-key, GM-first, World-Turn-led) survives every lens I can throw at it. The adjustments: (1) Trust Package expands to include M1 + M2 + honor-ware (Ruling 5/6); (2) launch sequencing is embargoed until World Turn + Trust ship (Ruling 2/4); (3) the demo is deterministic-first, AI-second (Ruling 8); (4) a named distribution plan with founder-time budgeted becomes a first-class workstream (§5). With those, this is the rare solo product where the moat is architectural (offline single-file + deterministic engine — structurally unavailable to SaaS rivals without rebuilding their businesses) and the remaining work is completion, not invention (P-§7: ~70% built).
