## (a) Programmes that pay a Bangladesh-registered partner WITHOUT own PayPal or own Stripe (official page opened)
1. Gumroad affiliates and collaborators (row 9a, 9b): BDT bank deposit; min $100-equivalent standard; needs ID + proof of residence.
2. Impact.com (row 1): wire in BDT to a Bangladesh bank, $10 minimum; payment still depends on each brand approving you.
3. Lemon Squeezy affiliates (row 10): Bangladesh on bank-payout list, paid in USD, bi-monthly.
4. Paved (row 16b): BDT bank transfer; but $1,000 minimum and 2-month cycle; for publishers/newsletters only.
5. Shopify Collabs (row 11): probable (Hyperwallet via Shopify Partner payouts names Bangladesh, Nov 2021 blog); re-verify on a current page before relying.
Conditional (merchant chooses the rail, so Bangladesh depends on the merchant): Tapfiliate (Payoneer and international bank are named methods), FirstPromoter external/bulk route, UpPromote manual, Refersion manual "mark as paid", Rewardful Managed Payouts "bank transfer, PayPal, Wise... depending on location".
Payoneer-named networks where the Bangladesh country list was NOT seen: CJ (Payoneer offered), Awin (Payoneer GBT), Admitad (Payoneer in Wallet). Plausible, not confirmed.

## (b) Surprising
- Gumroad now states the standard minimum payout is $100 USD (many sources still say $10), and says it does not support Payoneer, Wise, wire or check. Bangladesh is on the BDT bank table with no "(min X)" flag.
- beehiiv's partner programme moved to Dub (Stripe Express bank account or USDC wallet); older sources saying "PayPal only" are outdated. Bangladesh coverage on Dub not visible in the page text.
- Rakuten (search summary): Payoneer "not an available payment option"; direct deposit requires bank in same country as address.
- Amazon Associates bank transfer only for banks in the US, UK or Eurozone; Bangladesh bank not covered.
- Partnerize and Rakuten expose almost no country detail; PartnerStack (and so Kit) pages are behind 403.
- CommissionCrowd explicitly says it is only a facilitator; its Stripe Connect coverage was 39 countries (2020).
- Upwork: live commission-only listings exist, but the official policy text could not be read (blocked).
