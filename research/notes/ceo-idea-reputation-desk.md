# CEO idea (2026-10-30): "Shothik abroad" → restaurant reputation desk, open market

## The idea as the CEO gave it (voice, paraphrased)
- **Who:** ordinary mid-range restaurants (mid-low to mid-high) in the US, UK and other English-speaking countries. No Bangla or diaspora limit.
- **1. Watch reviews.** An agent checks for new negative reviews every hour (every 15 or 5 minutes for a higher fee). A human team member then responds and tries to win the unhappy customer back, for example with "50% off next time".
- **2. Keep the profile visible.** Update the Google profile weekly or monthly so the restaurant appears more in Google and in AI answers (ChatGPT, Google AI).
- **3. Target restaurants that care about their rating,** especially those rated 1–3 stars.
- **Reach:** business email and phone from Google listings. Finding contacts is easy; getting the message read is the hard part.
- **Question raised:** can we avoid needing the owner's Google login?

## Manager's reshaped version to test (in the M1 companion)
- **The human value is judgment, not speed:**
  - handle the hard 1–3 star reviews well, in public;
  - find the operational cause (e.g. the same delivery complaint 5 times);
  - send the owner a monthly report on what the bad reviews say;
  - make a genuine make-good offer to an unhappy customer only with **no ask about the review**.
- **Proof-first opener, no login needed:** analyse a restaurant's public reviews. Example:
  > "Your last 40 reviews: N mention X; M bad reviews have no reply; your hours differ on Google vs website."
- **Buyers to compare:**
  - single low-rated owners;
  - multi-location groups (5–50 branches);
  - restaurant marketing agencies, white-label;
  - online-ordering resellers.

  Countries: US, UK, CA, AU, IE, NZ.
- **Trust:** a manager invite (no password), and the owner can remove us at any time. **Pay:** Payoneer invoice after the first month.
- **Must answer:**
  - Who already pays for human review handling, and how much?
  - Do cold proof-first messages to restaurants get replies (evidence)?
  - Does the AI-suggested-reply feature kill the reply-writing value?
  - What is the real price per location below Birdeye and Reputation.com?
