# PART A — PATH TEST v2

## A1. Why the plain 0/1/2 test leaks (the holes)
1. **A score is a feeling, not evidence.** Nothing separates "we tested it" from "it sounds right".
2. **One number hides the broken part.** Read is really three things: delivered, noticed, and reaching the decision-maker. Trust is really three things: identity, competence and risk. A "1" never says which one failed.
3. **No hunt for hidden planes** (false 2s). "Pay = 2, Payoneer works" ignores whether *this buyer* will pay a Payoneer request.
4. **No hunt for clever routes** (false 0s). "Read = 0, Facebook goes to requests" ignores the maintainer, the middleman and the public-reply routes.
5. **Unknown is scored as 0 or 2 by mood.** One untested assumption can kill a good idea or pass a bad one.
6. **Things outside the 9 steps are missing:** capacity at 8 h/week, timezone, AI substitution, licences, halal, demand trend.
7. **It scores the destination, not the road from today.** A step is only "2" if the team can start it on Monday with what it has.

## A2. Gates (pass/fail before any scoring)
| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| G1 Halal | No interest-like returns, gambling, alcohol, adult content or deception; check the buyer's industry *and* our sponsors/partners. |
| G2 Licence | No licensed or regulated act by us (customs broker, immigration advice, legal advice, claims management, recruitment licence where it applies). We do checklists, records and data. |
| G3 Capacity | The first client fits 2 people × 8 h/week, and a **cap breaker** is named (AI volume, a middleman who sells, or other people delivering under our checks). |
| G4 AI substitution | AI will not do it end-to-end within 12–24 months, or the paid part is the human sign-off or presence. |
| G5 Demand trend | Dated 2025–26 evidence that the need is rising. |
| G6 Killed list | Not on the brief's killed list, unless new evidence and a different path are written down. |

## A3. The scoring card (one row per step)
For each step record five things:
- **Score** 0/1/2.
- **Basis:** **M** (a mechanism makes it true) or **H** (hope).
- **Evidence grade:**
  - **E3:** we did it ourselves (counted, sent, tested);
  - **E2:** VERIFIED or primary source;
  - **E1:** SEARCH-SUMMARY or VENDOR CLAIM;
  - **E0:** ESTIMATE.
- **Plane check:** passed, or failed with the named plane.
- **Route:** which A5 route, if any, changed the score.

**Rules**
- **R1 — A 2 must be earned.** A 2 needs basis M, evidence ≥ E1, and a passed plane check (A6). A 2 resting on H or E0 is capped at 1.
- **R2 — Hard vs soft 0.** A 0 kills only if it is **hard**: all A5 routes were tried and written down, and none lifts it. Otherwise it is a **soft 0**. It becomes a test (≤ 2 hours, $0) that must be run *before* the opportunity is ranked.
- **R3 — Chain.** The opportunity's score is its weakest step, written with its type, e.g. "1 (Read, soft, E1)". Among survivors, break ties by (a) the two-key count, then (b) the sum of steps scored M + ≥ E1.
- **R4 — Two-key rule.** At least **2 of Read, Trust and Pay** must be a 2 by mechanism. If not, the opportunity is flagged **weak by design**. It may survive, but it ranks below every opportunity that passes.
- **R5 — Split steps.** Read = the minimum of (Delivered, Noticed, Decision-maker reached). Trust = the minimum of (Identity, Competence, Risk). Pay = the minimum of (Rail works into Bangladesh, Buyer will use that rail, Collection risk).

## A4. What each step asks (with the false-2 trap to check)
| # | Step | Question for a 2 | Common false 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home | Can we make a convincing sample in ≤ 7 days from **public data only**, with no login, licence or cash? Write the hours. | The sample needs their data, or a paid tool. |
| 2 | Road | Have we **listed 20 real named buyers** (E3), and are 100+ findable the same way, each with a contact point? | "There are thousands" with no list or contact point. |
| 3 | Letter | Does line one show *their* problem or gain, with a number or screenshot? | A generic benefit dressed up as personal. |
| 4 | Read | Does the message land in a place the decision-maker checks: inbox (not spam or requests), platform proposal list, or a public thread they started? | Facebook/Instagram message requests; filtered vendor pitches; a role inbox nobody reads. |
| 5 | Impressed | Can they check the claim in 30 seconds **without trusting us**? | A claim that needs our tool or our word. |
| 6 | Reply | Is there a reason to answer *now* (a trigger) and an easy answer (a yes/no)? | "Free audit" with no deadline. |
| 7 | Trust | Identity: real, visible people? Competence: proof about their case? Risk: who loses if it fails? | "We're experienced" with zero track record; a guarantee we cannot honour. |
| 8 | Pay | Does money reach Bangladesh (Payoneer or platform), will *this* buyer use that rail, and who carries collection risk? | "Payoneer works" while the buyer only pays by Zelle, cash or PayPal. |
| 9 | Repeat | Does the need come back monthly or quarterly, or does a middleman multiply it? | A one-off fix sold as "monthly upkeep" nobody needs. |

## A5. False-0 finder: eight routes (try every one before calling a 0 "hard")
- **Ra — Change the buyer.** Sell to the maintainer, middleman, vendor or platform that serves many sufferers.
- **Rb — Change the channel.** A posted need, a platform, a public thread they started, a partner directory, a member list.
- **Rc — Change the proof.** Make it public and self-checkable: a screenshot pair, a benchmark, their own bot's words.
- **Rd — Change the price form.** Pay after proof, escrow, a fee from money recovered, or commission on new revenue.
- **Re — Change the timing.** A trigger event, a deadline, the hours after a post, a season (Ramadan/Eid, tax-year end).
- **Rf — Change the identity signal.** Shared language or faith, presence in Dhaka, a named human with a face and a video.
- **Rg — Change the unit.** One buyer with many end-clients instead of many single owners.
- **Rh — Change the country.** A timezone that fits Dhaka evenings, or an outreach law that allows the channel (UK limited companies and Australia instead of the EU).
If a route lifts the step to ≥ 1 with evidence ≥ E1, mark it **"false 0 — fixed by R_"** and rewrite the plan around that route.

## A6. False-2 finder: twelve planes (check every 2 against all of them)
| Plane | Test question |
|---|---|
| P1 Licence | Is any act regulated (customs, immigration advice, legal advice, claims management, recruitment)? |
| P2 Access | Do we need their login or private data *before* value is shown? |
| P3 Cash | Does it need paid Connects, a domain, calls, tools, travel or ads? |
| P4 Hours | Who does it, in which of their 8 hours a week? |
| P5 Timezone | Does a live step fall at 01:00 in Dhaka? |
| P6 Platform limits | WhatsApp non-reply cap, LinkedIn note quota, Connects, non-circumvention, message requests? |
| P7 Outreach law | CAN-SPAM, PECR (corporate vs sole trader), TPS/CTPS for calls, the Australian Spam Act, GDPR? |
| P8 Buyer-side rail | Will this buyer actually pay a Payoneer request or card link? |
| P9 Date movement | Has the deadline moved before, and was it re-confirmed this month? |
| P10 AI substitution | Can a product do this end-to-end within 12–24 months? |
| P11 Liability | Are we promising an outcome we cannot insure? |
| P12 Halal adjacency | Is the client's industry, the sponsor or the product halal? |
**The Monday test** settles each 2: name the exact first action and the asset it uses. If the asset is missing, that is a plane, and the 2 drops to a 1 or 0.

## A7. Worked example: Shothik (calibration case; known weak step = Read)
Shothik: fix mismatched hours and phone numbers across Google, website and delivery apps for Bangladeshi-owned restaurants in New York. Reach: the CEO messages the owner in Bangla on Facebook with a screenshot → free report → fix (≈ 1 h) → $100–150 via Payoneer → optional $60/month.

| Step | Plain score (v1 style) | v2 score (basis / evidence) | What v2 caught |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Home | 2 | **2** (M / E3-able) | Public data only, about 30 min per restaurant. Passes P2/P3. |
| 2 Road | 2 | **1** (H / E1) | No list of 20 named restaurants yet. "Nearly all NY Indian restaurants are Bangladeshi-owned" is an unverified claim (SEARCH-SUMMARY, 6sqft), and Curry Row fell from 30 to 6 (SEARCH-SUMMARY). A *false 2* until counted. |
| 3 Letter | 2 | **2** (M / E1) | Their own screenshot. |
| 4 Read | 1 | **1, soft** (H / E1) | Delivered: Facebook messages from non-friends go to Message Requests (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Meta help text). Noticed: unknown. Decision-maker: the page may be run by a relative. Google Business Profile chat no longer exists, ended 2024-07-31 (VERIFIED: Google developer page plus 9to5Google). Phoning at NY 15:00 is 01:00 in Dhaka (P5). **The known weak step is confirmed.** |
| 5 Impressed | 2 | **2** (M / E1) | Self-checkable: "your Google says closed Sunday, your site says open". |
| 6 Reply | 2 | **1** (H / E0) | No trigger: the problem is weeks old. Lifted by Re (send before Ramadan/Eid peaks or a holiday weekend). A *false 2*. |
| 7 Trust | 2 | **1** (Identity M, Competence M, Risk H) | Identity is strong (Bangla, real name). Competence is strong (the proof). Risk: the fix needs the owner to add us as a Google Business Profile manager, and many owners can't find their login (ESTIMATE). That is P2. A *false 2*. |
| 8 Pay | 2 | **1** (H / E1) | The Bangladesh side works (Payoneer). The buyer side is unknown: a card payment via a Payoneer request costs 3% (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Payoneer fee table), and small NY restaurants may prefer Zelle or cash, which cannot reach Bangladesh (ESTIMATE). P8. A *false 2*. |
| 9 Repeat | 1 | **1** (H / E0) | Hours change seasonally and on holidays, but $60/month upkeep is easy to cancel. |

**Result.** Plain test: chain = 1 (Read), and the idea looked one fix away. **v2: chain = 1 (Read, soft, E1), with four other 1s hidden behind false 2s** (Road, Reply, Trust-risk, Pay). Two-key rule: **fails**, because no part of Read, Trust or Pay is a 2 by mechanism. Verdict: **weak by design**. It survives, but ranks below any opportunity that passes two-key.

**What the route finder suggests (the useful part):**
- **Ra (change the buyer):** sell to whoever maintains many of these restaurants' listings, sites or ordering pages. Their access removes P2, and a B2B invoice improves Pay.
- **Rh (change the country):** the UK has ~8,000 curry houses that are 80–90% Bangladeshi-owned (SEARCH-SUMMARY), and UK 15:00 is 20:00–21:00 in Dhaka. Approach through their accountants by email: limited companies are reachable under PECR (brief).
- **Re (change the timing):** time sends 3–6 weeks before Ramadan (Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin around 8 Feb 2027; ESTIMATE, astronomical calculation) and Eid peaks.
- **Rf (change the identity signal):** a 30-second Bangla voice note or video after the first reply.
This is why M1 (maintainer route) and M8 (Affinity Desk via middlemen) are in the final set.

# PART B — REPLY ENGINEERING PLAYBOOK
**Goal:** make Read, Reply and Trust strong for two unknown people in Dhaka.
**Baseline to beat:** plain cold email averages about 3–5% replies (VENDOR CLAIM: Saleshandy 3.7% on 53.1M emails, CleanList 3.1%; notes §11; Ciela 2026 ≈ 3.4%, SEARCH-SUMMARY). Planning number: 100 targets → 3–5 replies → about 1 paying client (ESTIMATE). Every tactic below aims to double that, and is tested, not assumed.

## B1. Channel choice
- **T1 — Posted need first.** Go where the buyer already announced the need: job and contract posts, commission-rep listings, "advertise with us" pages, "looking for…" threads, platform requests. Such buyers exist and are public: podcasts post commission-only ad-sales roles at 20–30% (SEARCH-SUMMARY, podnews/cazvid listings). Read and Reply rise because the letter answers a question they asked (ESTIMATE).
- **T2 — Speed, with the right clock.**
  - *Inbound lead* (they contacted us): reply within 1 hour. The HBR 2011 audit of 2,241 US firms found responders within an hour were about 7× more likely to have a meaningful conversation with a decision-maker than responders an hour later (SEARCH-SUMMARY; the figure appears consistently across many secondary sources, but the article was not opened).
  - *Competitive bid* (Upwork): aim for **1–4 hours after posting, not the first 30 minutes**. One analysis of 59,339 proposals (Jan–Feb 2026) found the best reply rate, 24.3%, in the 2–4 h window and the worst in 0–30 min (VENDOR CLAIM, GigRadar via a write-up). About 59% of a job's proposals arrive in the first hour (VENDOR CLAIM, UpHunt).
  - Dhaka note: US-morning posts appear at about 19:00–23:00 in Dhaka, so keep one daily 30-minute evening window (ESTIMATE).
- **T3 — Sell to whoever serves many.** Maintainers, agencies, accountants, associations, screening firms. Their inbox is a *business* inbox that expects suppliers, and one yes multiplies (brief lesson: middlemen multiply; Read rises because supplier mail is expected; ESTIMATE).
- **T4 — Affinity channel, tested.** For diaspora targets, A/B test Bangla vs English on 20 + 20 sends. Expect a higher open and reply rate in Bangla from a Bangladeshi sender (ESTIMATE; no study found). Keep the content identical so the language is the only variable.
- **T5 — Public reply beats a stranger's DM.** Facebook messages from non-friends go to Message Requests (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Meta help text). A helpful public answer on a thread *they* started, or a comment on their own post, reaches their notifications (ESTIMATE). Answer first; offer the proof only when invited.

## B2. The exact contact point
- **T6 — Use the inbox that matches the offer:** partnerships@, vendor-onboarding forms, advertise@, a named owner on the "About" page. It is also the legal route in Australia: inferred consent covers an address **conspicuously published** in a work role, *if* the message relates to that role and no "no unsolicited messages" note sits beside it (SEARCH-SUMMARY, several Australian law-firm summaries of the Spam Act 2003; primary text not opened).
- **T7 — Find the maintainer from the footer.** "Website by…", platform badges, agency portfolio pages and case studies list which vendor serves which business. One email can then cover several broken clients (ESTIMATE).
- **T8 — Use pre-qualified lists:** partner directories, association member lists, screening-industry member directories, "advertise" pages. Everyone on them has already self-declared as a buyer or partner type (ESTIMATE).
- **T9 — Don't plan on dead doors.** Google Business Profile chat ended on 2024-07-31 (VERIFIED: Google developer page plus 9to5Google). Use the phone, site form or email listed instead.

## B3. Timing triggers
- **T10 — Contact on a trigger, never "just because":**
  - a new job or contract post (hours);
  - a new review naming the problem;
  - a deadline 6–10 weeks out (ESTIMATE);
  - an enforcement surge (UK restaurant illegal-working visits +31% in Jan–Jun 2026; VERIFIED, gov.uk note updated 7 Aug 2026, plus IBTimes);
  - a season: Ramadan and Eid for restaurants and charities, the UK tax-year end on 5 April for Gift Aid (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Fundraising UK);
  - a new location, funding round or product launch.
- **T11 — Arrive at the start of their day.** Use Gmail "schedule send" (free) so email lands at 08:00–10:00 recipient time (ESTIMATE; vendor "best time" claims conflict).

  | Market | Recipient 09:00 = Dhaka time | Live-contact window in Dhaka |
  |---|---|---|
  | UK | 14:00 (BST) / 15:00 (GMT) | UK 14:00–17:00 = Dhaka 19:00–23:00 ✔ (also a restaurant's quiet afternoon) |
  | US East | 19:00 (EDT) / 20:00 (EST) | US morning 09:00–12:00 = Dhaka 19:00–23:00 ✔ for offices. A restaurant's quiet afternoon (14:00–17:00) = Dhaka 00:00–04:00 ✘ |
  | Gulf (UTC+4) | 11:00 | Their whole working day ✔ |
  | Australia East | 04:00–05:00 | Their afternoon = Dhaka 08:00–13:00 ✔ |

  (Offsets are standard timezone arithmetic; daylight saving shifts the UK and US by 1 h.)
- **T12 — Re-confirm the date before any deadline message.** Indonesia moved its halal-certification deadline for imported products from 17/18 Oct 2026 to Oct 2028, days before it applied (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Emerhub, Tilleke & Gibbins, CIRS). One stale date makes us look careless.

## B4. Message form
- **T13 — Short, with one question.** Aim for 40–90 words, 1 question, and a 3–4-word subject. Basis: a Boomerang analysis of ~40M emails (2016) found 50–125 words and 1–3 questions did best (+50% vs no question), but it covered all email, not cold email (SEARCH-SUMMARY). Hunter.io recommends 20–39 words for cold email (VENDOR CLAIM). The 40–90 range is our ESTIMATE between the two.
- **T14 — Line one is their fact, not our introduction.** For example: "Your Google listing says closed Sundays; your website says 12–10." (ESTIMATE; the CEO's Letter step.)
- **T15 — An honest identity line**, in every first message: real names, "a two-person team in Dhaka, Bangladesh", and one link to a page with our faces. This is the halal no-deception rule *and* identity Trust (ESTIMATE). Hiding location is forbidden and would fail when discovered.
- **T16 — An easy answer.** End with a yes/no: "Want the one-page report? Reply 'yes'." Questions raise replies (Boomerang 2016, SEARCH-SUMMARY).
- **T17 — No links or attachments in the first cold email.** Put the checkable fact in the text and send the full proof after a reply. This protects deliverability (VENDOR CLAIM, common deliverability advice), and the fact in the text still makes Impressed work (ESTIMATE).

## B5. Proof format
- **T18 — The 30-second self-check.** The proof must be checkable without trusting us: their own listing, their own bot's answer, their own number. For example: "Ask your bot 'do you open Sunday?' It says yes; your site says no." (ESTIMATE; this is the Impressed test in A4.)
- **T19 — Short personal video for top targets only**, after a reply or for the top 10. Vendors claim a 2–4× reply lift (VENDOR CLAIM: Sendspark, FirstSales, Loom internal data via write-ups). No independent study was found. A/B test it.
- **T20 — The benchmark as letter (M1 scale mode):** "You're one of 120 we checked; here's your row." Being named in a set prompts a reply (ESTIMATE). Aggregate first, and offer a right of reply before publishing.
- **T21 — A public identity kit:**
  - a one-page site or Notion page with both names, faces, a Dhaka address and a dated sample;
  - a LinkedIn profile with real photos;
  - a 60-second intro video.
  This answers "are they real?" before it is asked (ESTIMATE).

## B6. Risk reversal (the Trust-risk part)
- **T22 — Pay after proof, small first ticket.** Fix first, invoice after, with a $100–300 written scope (ESTIMATE; brief lessons: Trust and Pay were weak everywhere).
- **T23 — Let the platform hold the money** when on Upwork (escrow for fixed-price contracts; Upwork feature, ESTIMATE label because the page was not opened this session).
- **T24 — A fee from the result:** commission on new revenue, or a % of money recovered. It is a halal reward for a result, and the buyer risks nothing. Buyers already accept it: the 20–30% commission-only ad-sales posts above (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
- **T25 — A re-test promise, not a guarantee.** "If anything we signed off breaks, we re-test free within 7 days." That controls liability (P11) while still reversing risk (ESTIMATE).

## B7. Channel rules and limits (P6/P7)
- **T26 — Email law by country.**
  - US CAN-SPAM: true identity, a valid physical postal address and an opt-out (brief).
  - UK PECR: limited companies and LLPs are fine with identity and opt-out; sole traders and partnerships need consent (brief; ICO B2B guidance, SEARCH-SUMMARY).
  - Australia: see T6.
  - EU: skip cold email.
  Check each target's legal form before sending to UK businesses (Companies House is free).
- **T27 — No UK cold calls without screening.** Live B2B marketing calls are allowed only if the number is not on the TPS, or the CTPS for corporate subscribers (ICO guidance, SEARCH-SUMMARY). Screening costs money (P3). The US allows manual calls to business lines more freely (ESTIMATE; verify TCPA before any call campaign).
- **T28 — WhatsApp only after a reply.** WhatsApp has been testing a monthly cap on messages to people who have not replied (individuals and businesses), with the threshold undisclosed (VERIFIED as a test: TechCrunch 2025-10-17 plus several outlets). 2026 rollout status NOT FOUND.
- **T29 — LinkedIn notes are scarce.**
  - Free accounts get a small monthly quota of personalised invitation notes: 3–5 per month reported, sources conflict (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
  - Notes are capped near 200 characters.
  - New accounts get about 20–30 invites a week (VENDOR CLAIM, brief).
  Use notes only for the top 3 targets a month. For the rest, engage with their post first, then send a blank invite.
- **T30 — Upwork economics.** 10 free Connects a month on Basic, then $0.15 each. The service fee has been variable at 0–15% since 2025-05-01 (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Upwork resource page plus blogs). Taking a client off-platform within 24 months needs a conversion fee (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Upwork support). Bid on 1–3 posts a month, chosen hard.
- **T31 — Gmail hygiene.** Bulk-sender rules (authentication, one-click unsubscribe) bite at 5,000+ messages a day to Gmail (SEARCH-SUMMARY, third-party guides). We send ≤ 20 a day by hand (ESTIMATE), in plain text, honour every opt-out within 2 days, and keep spam complaints near zero (Google's exact thresholds were not verified this session).
- **T32 — Facebook.** Non-friends land in Message Requests (SEARCH-SUMMARY). Use public comments, community groups (follow their rules), or ask a mutual for an introduction. Never use a fake profile or pretend to be a customer.
- **T33 — Communities** (Reddit, Facebook groups, Skool, tool forums). Read the self-promotion rules, answer first, and send no unsolicited DMs (ESTIMATE). A useful public answer is also public proof for later buyers.

## B8. Measuring and stopping
- **T34 — Test in small, honest batches.** Run 2 variants × 30 sends and change one variable at a time. If both variants get **zero** positive replies across 60 sends, change the *route* (buyer, channel, trigger), not the wording. Why: at a 4% baseline, 60 sends with zero replies would happen by chance only about 9% of the time (0.96^60 ≈ 0.086; ESTIMATE from the vendor baseline). Track replies, not opens: open-tracking pixels hurt deliverability and are unreliable (ESTIMATE).

## B10. What makes two unknown people in Dhaka trusted (summary)
1. **Be checkable:** real names, faces, a Dhaka address, a dated public sample (T15, T21).
2. **Show, don't claim:** the buyer's own case, checkable in 30 seconds (T18).
3. **Take the risk:** pay after, a fee from results, a platform escrow (T22–T24).
4. **Borrow trust:** platforms, middlemen who already use offshore partners, shared language or faith (T1, T3, T4).
5. **Make Dhaka an asset where it is one:** presence (M6 checks on the ground) and native Bangla (M8). Never hide it.
