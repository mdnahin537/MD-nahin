# Brief — Phase 1 Opus Companion: attack, extend and finalise the business models

## STEP 0 — THE BRIEF

### 1. Role
You are a peer-level strategist, the manager's sparring partner. You have built offshore service businesses from zero and know how foreign buyers find, trust, hire and pay remote teams. You are expert at spotting loopholes, hidden failure points, new patterns and unusual openings that others miss. You lead this piece of work; the manager reviews it.

### 2. What I'm asking you to do
1. Attack the manager's draft of 13 business models in `research/models.md`. For each one, say what is wrong, what is missing, and what is a hidden "plane" (a step this team cannot take).
2. Add at least 3 new models that are not in the draft. They must be creative, realistic and aligned with this team.
3. Merge, cut, reshape and select a **final set of 7–9 models**. It must include M1 Problem Spotter, M2 Replacement Desk and M3 Idea Partner, though you may reshape them. Overwrite `research/models.md` with the final version.
4. Write `research/method.md` with two tools every Phase 2 companion will use:
   - (a) **Path Test v2**: an intelligence-led way to score the 9 steps. It must catch "false 0s" (a step that looks impossible but has a clever route) and "false 2s" (a step that looks easy but hides a plane). The CEO says the plain 0/1/2 test is "full of holes".
   - (b) **Reply Engineering Playbook**: concrete, evidence-backed ways to make Read, Reply and Trust strong for two unknown people in Dhaka. Cover channel, exact contact point, timing triggers, message form, proof format, risk reversal, and channel rules and limits. Every line needs evidence or the label ESTIMATE.

Why, in one line: Phase 2 will spend one Opus companion per model searching for real opportunities. A weak model set or a weak scoring method wastes all of that.

### 3. Context
**The team**
- MD Nahin (CEO), in Dhaka. Strong at strategy, research and ideas; good spoken English; weak at long repetitive work; doesn't code (AI writes any code, and that is OK).
- A partner who is excellent at exact, checklist-driven focused work and works in English.
- Both are part-time, about 8–35 hours a week each (design for 8).
- Zero clients and zero track record.
- Cash budget is about $0 beyond AI subscriptions (Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, Gemini Pro).

**Channels:** WhatsApp, Facebook (the CEO's personal account), email, and new LinkedIn and Upwork accounts with no history. Any other realistic route is also allowed.

**Payment:** Payoneer works. PayPal is uncertain in Bangladesh: not live as of September 2026; a Bangladesh Bank framework dated 2026-07-29 is reported; this is being checked separately. Wise and Stripe are not available.

**Halal limits:** no interest-like guaranteed returns, gambling, alcohol, adult content, or deception (no fake reviews, fake identities, or hiding that we are in Dhaka). Profit-share and pay-for-result fees are fine.

**Out:** local Bangladeshi businesses as final buyers. Also killed already, so do not repeat:
- Shopify product uploads, AI video ads, UK landlord register, security questionnaires for US SaaS;
- dental lead gen, generic research/data work, bookkeeping, e-commerce support automation;
- website accessibility fixes, fixing apps built with AI tools, course production, wholesaler order entry;
- generic "AI chatbot/automation agency", grant writing, construction measurement, translation checking;
- Google Shopping feeds, insurance-certificate tracking, US government bids, review-reply writing on its own.

You may revisit one of these only with strong new evidence and a different path, and you must say why.

**The CEO's 9-step path test** (a chain; an opportunity is only as strong as its weakest step):
1. Home: a convincing free sample in 7 days, no coding by us, no licence, ~$0.
2. Road: reach 100+ buyers.
3. Letter: the first message shows their own problem or gain.
4. Read: they actually see it.
5. Impressed: the value is obvious in seconds.
6. Reply: a reason to answer now.
7. Trust: why trust two unknown people in Dhaka?
8. Pay: money reaches Bangladesh.
9. Repeat: recurring need and referrals.

The plain score is 0/1/2 per step, and any 0 kills the opportunity.

**Work level:** medium to medium-high. Not a 10-minute job for a person or AI, and not beyond what the team can learn with AI.

**Demand:** must be rising in 2025–2026 with dated evidence, ideally because of AI. Rank down anything AI will do end-to-end without a human within 1–2 years.

**Lessons**
- Cheap labour is not an edge.
- Single small-business owners pay about $100–250/month; middlemen multiply value.
- Read, Trust and Pay were weak on almost every road tested.
- Marketplaces fix Trust and Pay but are crowded.
- Facebook messages from strangers land in "message requests".
- WhatsApp reportedly limits messages to non-repliers.
- New LinkedIn accounts get about 20–30 invites a week (vendor figure).
- Email: US CAN-SPAM allows B2B email with true identity, postal address and opt-out. UK: limited companies are OK with identity and opt-out; sole traders need consent. Mostly skip the EU.

**Baseline to beat, "Journey Shothik" (M1):**
- What it is: fix mismatched opening hours and phone numbers across Google, the website and delivery apps for Bangladeshi-owned restaurants in New York.
- Path: the CEO messages the owner in Bangla on Facebook with a screenshot → free report → fix ≈ 1 hour → $100–150 via Payoneer → optional $60/month.
- Weak step: Read (message requests).

**Evidence already gathered:** read `research/notes/phase1-research.md` first, and don't redo it.

**Manager's draft:** `research/models.md`.

### 4. Rules
- **You cannot launch agents yourself (tested).** If you need a helper, write a precise helper brief to `research/handoffs/phase1-helper-N.md`, then end your turn with a final message whose first line is exactly `NEEDS_HELP: research/handoffs/phase1-helper-N.md`. The manager launches the helper and sends you the result path; you then continue with your memory intact.
  - Use a **Sonnet** helper for evidence collection.
  - Use a **Haiku** helper for single exact facts.
  - Put the model and effort level you want on the first line of the helper brief (e.g. `MODEL: sonnet | EFFORT: high`).
  - Use at most 3 helper rounds in total; usage is limited.
- **Web:** web SEARCH works. Web page FETCH was blocked on 2026-10-29. Test one fetch; if it works, use it, and for YouTube use `https://youtube-transcript.ai/transcript/{VIDEO_ID}.txt`. If it fails, rely on search and label those claims `SEARCH-SUMMARY`.
- **Labels:** label every key claim VERIFIED (confirmed by an independent source), VENDOR CLAIM, SEARCH-SUMMARY, or ESTIMATE (your reasoning). Never invent numbers, names, prices, posts or sources; write NOT FOUND plus what you tried.
- **Checkpoint:** save `research/models.md` and `research/method.md` as you go, so a usage-limit stop loses little. Put a line `STATUS: IN PROGRESS — next: …` at the top until you finish, then `STATUS: FINAL`.
- **Each final model must include:**
  - what we sell;
  - who pays;
  - how we find and reach them;
  - the first step from home (Monday-doable);
  - the money path from a $100–300 first job to $1,000+/month per client or many repeat clients;
  - which path steps are strong or weak by design, and why;
  - how AI delivers and what the human is responsible for;
  - **3–6 hunting grounds** (domains or buyer types) for Phase 2 to search, with a one-line reason each;
  - the planes to watch.
- **Final message to the manager:** at most 300 words. The final model list (one line each), what you cut and why, the 3 most important ideas in method.md, and anything you could not verify.

### 5. Tips
- Think from the buyer's side. Who already pays outside help for this today? Where do they already look for help?
- The best models make the weak steps (Read, Trust, Pay) strong **by mechanism**, for example:
  - the buyer posted the need;
  - a platform holds the money;
  - the proof is public;
  - the fee comes only from money recovered;
  - the middleman already trusts offshore partners.
- **New-category parity:** in a category younger than about 18 months, nobody has a track record, so this team's "zero track record" matters less. Look for these.
- Look at what real small teams worldwide actually do in 2025–2026: AI-native service firms, offshore teams in Nepal, Pakistan, Kenya and the Philippines, and productized services. Look for patterns that fit this team.
- Be ruthless with the manager's draft; it is a first pass.

### 6. What not to do

- Don't pick a destination and assume a road exists.

- Don't search for specific opportunities in depth; that is Phase 2. You may use examples to test a model.

## STEP 1 — WHY THIS MATTERS
For weeks, ideas died because the price was tiny and the market crowded, or because there was no realistic road for two unknown people in Dhaka to reach and win the buyer. Business models give the search a structure, so the team knows what money-making machine it is building. A wrong model set sends Phase 2's expensive research down dead roads. A missed creative model throws away the team's best start. Total market size, impressive technology and clever-sounding ideas don't matter. Real buyers do: reachable from where the team stands, with a growing need, paying for medium-high work, and coming back.
