## 4. First money: the script-tag deadline desk (Nov 2026 – Mar 2027)
- **What we sell:**
  1. **A free public scanner:** "paste your store URL → which of your apps stop working on 1 March". It reads public storefront pages, as Part B did, and is the lead magnet.
  2. **To app developers with old install bases:** a merchant-activation desk that walks each merchant through switching to the new app embed (or does it with collaborator access), billed **per merchant switched**. Price: test $5–10 (ESTIMATE, untested).
  3. **To merchants:** a $79 audit-and-fix (ESTIMATE). For apps whose developer has vanished, we point them to a replacement app (later, ours).
- **Why it's real:**
  - 14 of 21 big brand stores sampled still load script tags (B, VERIFIED raw pages);
  - one developer says 70–75% of its users still depend on them (VENDOR CLAIM);
  - no incumbent desk or audit app was found (B counter-search).
- **Why it fits:** it is mostly support and process work, which suits the partner's checklists and Dhaka hours. And it teaches the exact technology (app embeds) that app #1 is built on.
- **Weakens it** (B counter-evidence):
  - Shopify may extend the date (it extended Scripts before) or add bulk-enable;
  - some exposed stores are dead;
  - big vendors will migrate their own merchants.

  Our buyers are therefore mid-size developers with old installs, and merchants running abandoned apps.
- **Money guess (ESTIMATE, all inputs guesses):** 2 developer clients × 800 merchants × $5 ≈ $8k, plus 30 audits × $79 ≈ $2.4k. Realistic floor: $0–2k.
- **Pay rail:** Payoneer invoice, or Upwork for merchant jobs (both VERIFIED working, round 1).
- **Fallback if the sprint gets no buyer by day 45:** sell the same skill as Upwork jobs on the CEO's existing profile (one honest profile).
