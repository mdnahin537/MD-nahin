## 1. Top pick (2 lines)
**Release QA desk (M2).** We answer posted manual-testing jobs (Upwork first). Each proposal includes a short video of a real bug we found in the buyer's own live app. After that, a named person signs off each release. **Run the Gift Aid back office (reframed M8) alongside it** as a low-effort second test.

**Confidence:** **medium** that this is the right *first test*; **low** that it reaches $1,000/month by month 6 (critic's mid estimate: about $300/month at month 6).

**The honest headline:** none of the 29 opportunities is a strong business on paper today. For two unknown people in Dhaka, the binding constraint is whether anyone reads and replies, and only a test can answer that. The top picks are the cheapest, fastest honest tests, not proven businesses.

## 4. The top pick's full path: Release QA desk (with the critic's fixes)
| Step | Score | How it works / why this score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Home | 2 | A 45-minute exploratory test of a public app from a live job post, recorded with a free screen recorder. No login, no coding, $0. | The method (M2 file) |
| 2 Road | 2 | Posted QA jobs on Upwork (count still to be measured). 30 QA roles open to Bangladesh on Himalayas on 2026-10-29. | E3 count (M2) |
| 3 Letter | 2 | Line one is *their* bug: "Your checkout button fails on Android Chrome; 40-second video attached." | Method |
| 4 Read | **1** | A zero-review profile may never be opened. The profile itself may be refused as "too crowded". | SEARCH-SUMMARY (Upwork forum, 2016–2020) |
| 5 Impressed | 2 | A real, reproducible bug in their own product is checkable in seconds. | Method |
| 6 Reply | **1** | Crowded bidding. GigRadar's Q2 2026 data shows a 5.1% average reply rate *for experienced agencies*. | VENDOR CLAIM (GigRadar) |
| 7 Trust | 2 | Proof first (the bug), Upwork escrow, real names and faces, "Dhaka, Bangladesh" stated. | Mechanism |
| 8 Pay | 2 | Upwork escrow → withdraw to a Bangladeshi bank (about $0.99 per withdrawal) or Payoneer. | SEARCH-SUMMARY (Upwork help blocked) |
| 9 Repeat | 1 | Paid per release when the client ships, realistically $150–300 a pass. A $500/month retainer is unsupported. | Critic; GigRadar median $74.62 for jobs under $500 |

**Chain:** 1 (Read/Reply). **Two-key:** 2/3 PASS.

**Demand** (at least 2 dated points):
- **Rising:**
  - QA testing +9% (Sep 2025) and +19% (Nov 2025) in Upwork's own releases (VERIFIED, syndicated copies);
  - +20% month on month in June 2026 (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
- **Against:**
  - Upwork's total gross services volume was −4% in Q2 2026, and active clients fell from 796k to 763k (VERIFIED, Upwork Q2 2026 results via Finviz);
  - Upwork job posts fell 14.2% from Q1 to Q2 2026, the fourth straight quarterly fall (VENDOR CLAIM, GigRadar).

**AI risk:** AI coding tools now test their own apps (Replit Agent 3 self-tests in a browser, VERIFIED). That pushes the human value toward exploratory testing and a named sign-off, which is our product.

**Money path:**
1. First job $100–300.
2. Then 4–6 repeat clients at $150–300 per release pass, which is about $1,000/month.
3. The cap breaker: Dhaka testers we have verified in person do the volume under our checklist (the #4 bench).

At ~3–5 h per pass, 6 passes a month fits the ~70 h/month two people have (ESTIMATE).

**Critic's fixes, now part of the plan:**
1. **One honest Upwork profile per founder** (or an Upwork Agency). Never two people on one profile; that would also be deception (halal rule).
2. **Test with 20 bug-video proposals over 2–3 weeks,** not 5 in 7 days. At a 5% reply rate, 5 sends return nothing 77% of the time.
3. **Start the crowdtesting door in week 1,** after checking that it pays to Bangladesh.
4. **Screen every *app*,** not just the company: no loan, betting or adult-adjacent apps.

**Go / stop numbers:**
- **GO:** ≥ 15 suitable posts a week, ≥ 2 reproducible bugs per 45-minute pass (confirmed by the other founder), and ≥ 1 reply or contract offer within 20 proposals.
- **STOP or re-route to crowdtesting:** the profile is refused twice, or 0 replies after 20 proposals.

For never-recognised ones, sell only the start-up pack and quote future Gift Aid, never a backlog. |

3. **CEO, 30 min, a decision:** approve a small **Connects budget** (Connects are Upwork's paid bid credits). 20 proposals over 2–3 weeks likely cost about **$25–50** (ESTIMATE). That is above the brief's $20 test ceiling, so it is your call.
   - Upwork lists Connects at $0.15 each, with up to 10 free a month on Basic accounts. New accounts often don't get the 10 (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
   - At the market-average reply rate, a first conversation costs about $50 of Connects (VENDOR CLAIM, GigRadar). **The bug video must beat the average 2–3× to pay for itself.** That is exactly what the test measures.

   **In the same week, low effort:** the partner lists 10 Ltd/LLP examiner accountants and 10 recognised charities whose accounts show Gift Aid in past years but none recently. Then send 10 organisation-domain emails plus 5 posted letters.
