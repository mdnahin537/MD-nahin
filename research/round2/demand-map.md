## 2. Investors now back "AI-native services" (services sold like software)
- Y Combinator's Spring 2026 Request for Startups includes AI-native agencies (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Monetizely excerpt).
- Sequoia: roughly $6 is spent on services for every $1 on software (SEARCH-SUMMARY, quoted on a Globant page).

**Reading:** a leveraged service company (AI does most of the work, humans check it, cheap Bangladesh operations behind it) is now a fundable shape, not a "cheap labour" story.

## 3. AI agent security and governance (mostly enterprise)
- 83% of enterprises have deployed or plan AI agents; only 29% feel ready to secure them (Cisco 2026, via a blog: SEARCH-SUMMARY).
- Agentic-AI security market: ~$2.43B in 2026 → $9.63B in 2031 (Mordor via Nightfall, VENDOR CLAIM).
- MCP-security market (MCP is the standard way AI agents connect to tools): ~$134M (2025) → ~$633M (2031) (TechSci, VENDOR CLAIM).

**Reading:** growing fast but enterprise-led. That means long sales cycles and big-vendor competition (Palo Alto, CrowdStrike, Check Point bought Lakera).
