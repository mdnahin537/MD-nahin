## 4. Odds
- **Strongest failure reason:** Trust plus substitutes. Free AUSTRAC starter kits cover small, low-complexity firms (the exact target), and local SaaS vendors give the program, training and support for A$179/month with no set-up fee. A done-for-you program from Dhaka has no price room and the weakest trust position in a compliance sale.
- **Counter-evidence search run:** "AUSTRAC tranche 2 regulatory approach first year … enforcement priorities". Result: AUSTRAC targets firms that "have not enrolled" or show no "meaningful effort" (SEARCH-SUMMARY, AUSTRAC 2025-26 priorities). So urgency is real for laggards; it does not help an unknown seller. The other side: AUSTRAC's kit page says firms cannot rely on the kit alone, so help is needed (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
- Odds for us: **very low (~5%, ESTIMATE).**

## 8. Halal and licence
- Halal: helping firms obey anti-money-laundering law is halal. Screen clients: no gambling venues; jewellers/bullion dealers are fine if they trade spot.
- Licence/liability: no licence to consult, but the **independent evaluation** must be done by an independent, suitably qualified person, and drafting programs for law firms edges toward legal advice. A wrong program exposes the client to penalties: an uninsurable promise for us (plane P11).
