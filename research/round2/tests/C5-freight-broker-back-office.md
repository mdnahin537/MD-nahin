## 2. Demand trend (dated)
- Active brokerage authorities: **28,438, −7.8% YoY** (Brush Pass Research via FreightCaviar, ~early 2024); net −265 brokerages in **June 2024**, sixth straight monthly fall. SEARCH-SUMMARY.
- New authorities published fell by **>50%** after FMCSA made identity verification mandatory in **April 2025**. SEARCH-SUMMARY (FreightWaves).
- Fraud pressure up: Highway blocked **527,000+ fraudulent inbound emails in Q1 2026, ~+50% YoY**. VENDOR CLAIM (Highway index via a DEV.to write-up).
- Reading: pain per broker is rising; the number of brokers is falling.

## 3. Size if it works (ESTIMATE, bottom-up)
- Reachable buyers: ~28k brokerages (SEARCH-SUMMARY); share that would hand ops to an offshore AI+human desk found through Upwork or referrals ≈ 1% → ~280.
- Price: outcome or seat pricing must sit near the offshore VA market ($4–8/h ≈ $700–1,400/month per seat, SEARCH-SUMMARY) or per-load fees of ~$5–10 (ESTIMATE).
- **Year 1 ceiling:** 4 brokers × $1,000/month × 6 months ≈ **$24k**.
- **Year 3 ceiling:** 80 brokers × $1,500/month × 12 ≈ **$1.4M/yr** (the only big ceiling among C1–C6), but it needs ~60–80 US-hours staff in Dhaka, and margin is set by Philippine and Indian rivals.

## 4. Odds
- **Strongest failure reason:** squeezed between software and cheaper labour. AI agents (FleetWorks' AI carrier reps; Overroute, $5.5M seed, for track-and-trace and check calls; Chain; Peer, an AI-native brokerage in YC Summer 2026) automate the tasks, and TMS/load boards build vetting in. What remains is staffing at $4–8/hour. Trust is worst exactly where the value is (fraud checks).
- **Counter-evidence search run:** "freight brokerage AI agents startups carrier vetting track and trace automation 2026 …". Result: many funded AI entrants and built-in vetting (above). No evidence that brokers prefer an AI+offshore desk over software plus their own staff.
- Odds: **low (~8%, ESTIMATE).**

- Software/AI: Highway (fraud index, vetting), DAT Carrier Management Suite, Transfix built-in vetting, FleetWorks, Overroute, Chain, HappyRobot (funding NOT checked). SEARCH-SUMMARY.

## 8. Halal and licence
- Halal: freight is halal. **Grey:** brokers move alcohol and pork loads among others; and **freight factoring** (selling invoices at a discount with interest-like fees) is common in billing follow-up. Rule: no factoring work; screen brokers whose main lanes are alcohol.
- Licence: back-office support needs no FMCSA authority as long as we never arrange loads in our own name (that would need FMCSA broker authority and a surety bond; the bond amount was not verified this session).
