## 4. Odds
- **Strongest failure reason:** the flood. 51% of apps have zero reviews, 81% have fewer than 10, and only ~36% seem to earn anything (VENDOR CLAIM, RevenueHunt June 2026). Obvious AI niches fill within months: PO/email-to-draft-order already has ≥ 7 apps (LevelOps, Quote Desk, POinbox, DraftFlow, POdraft, AutoApprove, Kaison) (SEARCH-SUMMARY). Compliance niches have little store demand (Belgian Peppol 160 installs, VERIFIED; Saudi ZATCA apps 0–4 reviews, SEARCH-SUMMARY).
- **Counter-evidence searches run:** (1) RevenueHunt "state of the Shopify app economy" (above). (2) "Shopify partner payout Bangladesh Hyperwallet … problem": no Bangladesh failure found; one Nigerian partner could not activate Hyperwallet, and staff sent him to support (SEARCH-SUMMARY, community.shopify.dev). Shopify's partner payout help exists in Bengali (SEARCH-SUMMARY; page title "পেমেন্ট পাওয়া").
- What improves the odds (VENDOR CLAIM, RevenueHunt): load-bearing categories win; 58% of localization apps clear 10+ reviews versus 11% in third-party logistics; their conclusion is that "distribution and trust" through support and reviews is the AI-proof move.
- Odds (ESTIMATE): ~60% of a first paying merchant within 4 months; ~20% of $10k MRR within 24 months; ~5% of a top-1% app.

## 7. Competition and AI substitution
- Competition: 22.5k apps and ~500 new a month (VENDOR CLAIM). AI coding lowers our cost and everyone else's.
- Platform build-in: Shopify keeps adding native features (native B2B now beyond Plus; Flow; Sidekick, not checked this session). Rule (d) above excludes niches Shopify is visibly building.
- Moat we can actually hold: support speed (Dhaka staff cover US nights), done-for-you set-up, reviews earned one merchant at a time, and a portfolio cross-promoting itself (the Storeware pattern). Software alone is not a moat.

## 8. Halal and licence
- Halal: general merchant tooling is permitted under the brief. Never build features specific to haram trades (alcohol age-gates, betting, interest-based "buy now pay later" add-ons, adult); do not market to those verticals. Shopify's 2.9% is a processing fee, not interest. Flag: some installers will sell haram goods; a general tool is acceptable, but the CEO may add a terms-of-use exclusion.
- Licence: none. Shopify requires mandatory privacy (GDPR) webhooks and app review compliance; merchant data stays within app scopes.
