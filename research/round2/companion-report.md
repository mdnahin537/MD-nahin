## 2. Recommended company: R1, a Dhaka app company on the Shopify App Store
**What it sells:** small, load-bearing Shopify apps, built with AI coding, each with:
- a free plan;
- a ~$19/month plan;
- a one-time **"we set it up for you in 24 hours"** charge, done by Dhaka staff.

**Why this one:**
1. **Buyers are already inside the store.**
2. **Shopify bills them.** Merchants pay on their Shopify bill, so we need no Stripe or PayPal account.
3. **Shopify pays partners in Bangladesh.**
4. **A Dhaka team has done it.**

The moat is not code, which AI made cheap for everyone. The moat is:
- niche choice by rule;
- fast human set-up and support;
- reviews earned one merchant at a time;
- later, a portfolio of apps that promote each other.

**The ladder:**
- **Rung 1:** app #1 plus paid set-ups.
- **Rung 2:** 3–5 apps, "Built for Shopify" status, partner referrals.
- **Rung 3:** WooCommerce ports through WordPress.org, billed through Lemon Squeezy, then Dhaka support hires. This is the WPDeveloper/weDevs path.

**Monday check (2 Nov 2026):**
- **First action:** the partner creates a free Shopify Partner account and opens the payout settings to see whether Hyperwallet bank transfer to Bangladesh is offered. Assets needed: an email, ID and a Bangladeshi bank account. The team has all three.
- **Missing asset:** about $30–40 in cash: the $19 App Store registration plus ~$10–20 of LLM API credit. **The CEO must approve this; it is above $0.** Nothing else is missing.

## 3. The three facts that most decide it
1. **Pay works without our own Stripe or PayPal.**
   - Shopify's partner payouts through Hyperwallet name **Bangladesh** among the countries covered, by local bank transfer or wire. **VERIFIED** at the primary page (shopify.com/partners/blog/payout-methods; the rollout began 2 Nov 2021, so the date is old; re-check in the dashboard).
   - Developers keep **100% of the first $1,000,000** of lifetime app revenue, then 85%. Registration is a **$19** one-time fee. **VERIFIED** (shopify.dev revenue-share page).
2. **Unknown Bangladeshi teams have won on this road.**
   - Storeware's apps have **772, 431, 158 and 56 reviews**, all "Built for Shopify". **VERIFIED** (apps.shopify.com/partners/storeware).
   - Storeware sits under Startise, parent of "Dhaka-based" WPDeveloper. **VERIFIED** (Future Startup, 2024-02-25).
   - WPDeveloper's Essential Addons has **1,000,000** active installs. **VERIFIED** (api.wordpress.org).
3. **The store is flooded and top-heavy, so the niche rule and support decide the outcome.**
   - Of **22,546** live apps, 51% have zero reviews, and the top 1% take about 72% of revenue. **VENDOR CLAIM** (RevenueHunt, June 2026 snapshot; page opened).
   - The obvious AI niche "PO email → draft order" already has ≥ 7 apps (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
   - Compliance apps draw little demand in stores: Belgian Peppol plugins have **160 installs** (VERIFIED); Saudi ZATCA Shopify apps have 0–4 reviews (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
