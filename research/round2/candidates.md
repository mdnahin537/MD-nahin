# Round 2 — Candidate companies (imagination pass)
**Rules for every candidate (from the CEO's round-2 brief):**
- It is B2B: businesses (small to big) or agencies.
- It is aimed at US/CA, UK/IE, EU, Gulf or AU/NZ.
- It **names its route to customers before anything else**.
- It is a ladder:
  - **Rung 1** brings in money within 3 months at $0 cash.
  - **Rung 2** is the scalable company: a product, partners, or AI plus Bangladesh operations.
- It is halal in what we sell and do.

Edges we use: the CEO's AI-agent and automation skills; AI-written code; English; live calls when needed; Bangladesh operations cost (~20k BDT for a strong hire) as a margin edge, never as the pitch; and new payment rails (Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad pay Bangladesh, see `demand-map.md` §7).

## Not shortlisted (one line each)
- **AI agent reliability/evals SaaS:** round 1 found built-in testing (Retell, Vapi) and free audits. G4 risk stays high.
- **UK MTD bridging software:** a huge wave (~1.08M in Apr 2027) but free and cheap tools plus incumbents (Xero, FreeAgent). Low price; weak edge.
- **UAE e-invoicing SME connector:** providers do it in-house (round 1 critic); Zoho/Tally integrate natively.
- **US AI-hiring-law compliance kits:** legal advice territory; Colorado's law is in flux; buyers want lawyers.
- **Agent security for enterprises:** right trend, wrong buyer for a two-person start (long enterprise cycles, big vendors).
- **Programmatic SEO/free-tool networks:** used as a *tactic* inside candidates, not a company.

## What the companion must decide for each shortlisted candidate
1. **Route proof:** is there evidence that buyers can be reached by this route by unknown sellers? (posted demand counts, marketplace listing rules, partner programs, reply evidence)
2. **Demand trend:** ≥ 2 dated points, earlier vs now.
3. **Size if it works:** the reachable buyer count × the realistic price → a ceiling for year 1 and year 3.
4. **Odds:** the strongest failure reason and a counter-evidence search.
5. **Rung 1 in ≤3 months at $0:** exact first offer, first 20 named targets, and first-money mechanics.
6. **Pay rail:** which rail pays us in Bangladesh, verified.
7. **Competition and AI substitution:** who already sells it, at what price, and whether the platform will build it in.
8. **Halal and licence check.**
