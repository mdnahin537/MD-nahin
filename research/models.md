# Business Models — DRAFT v1 (manager, 2026-10-29)
Status: DRAFT-INCOMPLETE. The Opus companion attacks this draft next. The final model set will replace it.
Evidence notes: research/notes/phase1-research.md

## The design rules every model must pass
These come from the failures so far.
1. **Edge is not cheap labour.** Any real edge comes from one of these:
   - (a) being physically in Bangladesh, or native in Bangla;
   - (b) accuracy plus a responsible human who signs off;
   - (c) speed from AI;
   - (d) **new-category parity**: in a category under ~18 months old, nobody has a track record, so "zero clients" hurts much less.
2. **Middlemen multiply.** One agency, manager or firm brings many end-clients.
3. **Read, Trust and Pay must be strong by design**, not by hope. Preferred routes:
   - the buyer already announced the need (job post, request, deadline);
   - a platform supplies trust and escrow;
   - proof first, pay after delivery;
   - a small first ticket.
4. **The first job needs no logins.** The proof can be made from the outside.
5. **Recurring by nature.** The need comes back every month or every event.
6. **Monday test.** Every first step must be doable next Monday with what the team has today.

## Candidate models (13; the companion must merge, cut or add, and Phase 2 runs one Opus companion per final model)

### M1 Problem Spotter (CEO)
- **We sell:** proof of a costly problem found from the outside → a fix → monthly "kept fixed".
- **Who pays:** an owner or manager who loses money because of the problem.
- **Find/reach:** public signals such as listings, sites, bots, ads and data. Message with the proof attached.
- **First step from home:** pick a niche and audit 20 businesses from the outside. Pick the 3 worst cases and make one-page proofs.
- **Money:** $100–300 first fix → $60–300/month upkeep → middlemen (agencies serving 20+ clients) → $1k+/month.
- **Path:** Home, Letter and Impressed are strong by design. Read and Trust are weak: a stranger says "you have a problem".
- **AI:** runs the scan, writes the report and drafts the fix; a human verifies every claim before sending.

### M2 Replacement Desk (CEO)
- **We sell:** a recurring job the client now pays staff or freelancers for, done faster, cheaper or more accurately, with a named human who is responsible for it.
- **Who pays:** firms with a visible repeated task (job ads for that role are the proof of demand).
- **Find/reach:** job posts and hiring pages. "You're hiring for X. We'll run X from next week, paid per unit, first batch free."
- **First step from home:** find live job ads for one task, then do a free sample batch from public material.
- **Money:** per unit or per month; one client can be $500–3,000/month.
- **Path:** Letter and Reply are strong (they announced the need). Trust is weak (handing a core job to strangers). AI risk is high where the task is fully automatable.
- **AI:** does the volume; the human does QA and owns the error log.

### M3 Idea Partner (CEO)
- **We sell:** a specific, evidence-backed growth opportunity for one client (a new channel, market, product line or data asset), then we run it with them.
- **Who pays:** owners with capacity but no time to research. Paid by fee plus a halal profit share (a share of real profit, never a guaranteed return).
- **Find/reach:** research finds gaps first, for example a client who sells on Amazon US but not on a channel where competitors grow.
- **First step from home:** build one "opportunity memo" per target, with numbers.
- **Money:** setup fee + % of profit from the new line; can reach $1k+/month per partner.
- **Path:** Impressed is strong. Trust and Pay are very weak: a profit share requires seeing their numbers, and strangers rarely partner. Likely a stage-2 upsell, not a door-opener.
- **AI:** market research, modelling and drafting; humans judge and run.

### M4 White-label Bench (sell to middlemen)
- **We sell:** delivery capacity to agencies, consultancies, IT providers and brokers who resell it under their own brand.
- **Who pays:** the middleman, who has 10–200 end-clients.
- **Find/reach:** agency directories, partner lists and agency job posts for "white-label partner". Email the owner with a done sample for one of their visible clients.
- **First step from home:** pick one service the middleman sells but struggles to deliver. Do it for one of their public clients as a sample.
- **Money:** per-client fee × their client count; one agency can be $1–5k/month.
- **Path:** Trust is helped (agencies are used to offshore partners). Repeat is strong. Weakness: crowded with Indian and Pakistani white-label shops, so it needs a niche nobody serves yet.
- **AI:** produces; the human does QA to the agency's standard.

### M5 Wave Desk (deadline-driven work)
- **We sell:** "get ready before date X". Fixed-price readiness work for a new rule, platform change or refund window, plus monitoring after.
- **Who pays:** businesses hit by the change who lack staff to handle it.
- **Find/reach:** public lists of who is affected (registries, marketplace sellers in the category, importers). The deadline gives a reason to reply now.
- **First step from home:** pick a wave with a real date, verify the date is still live, then build a readiness checklist + a sample for one real business.
- **Money:** fixed packages $200–1,500; waves end, so the model must roll into monitoring or the next wave.
- **Path:** Reply is strong by design (a deadline). Risks: deadlines move, licences may be needed (customs, legal), and waves are short.
- **Current waves found:** IEEPA tariff refunds (US, 2026; licence risk), EUDR (EU, 2026-12-30 / 2027-06-30), Bangladesh LDC graduation (2026-11-24).
- **AI:** reads the rules, maps them and drafts documents; the human checks every requirement.

### M6 Watchtower (monitoring subscription)
- **We sell:** continuous watching of something that changes and costs money when missed (prices, listings, policies, AI agents, brand abuse, compliance status), plus a monthly evidence pack and fixes.
- **Who pays:** brands, sellers, firms and their agencies.
- **Find/reach:** run the watch for one month for free on a public target; show what was caught.
- **First step from home:** set up a watch on 10 public targets and log findings for 7 days.
- **Money:** $100–500/month per watched entity; agencies multiply it.
- **Path:** Repeat is strong by design. Impressed is strong if the catch is real money. Software tools compete, so the edge is human-verified evidence plus action.
- **AI:** scans and drafts; the human verifies and acts.

### M7 Dhaka Ground Desk (presence edge)
- **We sell:** things only people physically in Bangladesh can do for foreign buyers: supplier finding and checking, factory visits with photo and video proof, sample handling, pre-shipment checks and compliance paperwork for small foreign brands buying from Bangladesh (apparel, leather, jute, home textiles).
- **Who pays:** small and medium foreign brands, often too small for big buying houses.
- **Find/reach:** brands that publicly ask "manufacturer in Bangladesh?" in forums, groups, LinkedIn and Upwork sourcing jobs.
- **First step from home:** build a verified shortlist of 10 small-MOQ factories in one category, with real photos and data.
- **Money:** fixed fee per supplier search ($150–500) + inspection fees + a commission on orders (a halal agency fee).
- **Path:** Trust is helped (buyers expect local agents; we show our real faces in Dhaka, and being in Dhaka is the point). Read is good (the buyer posts the need). Weak: part-time hours, travel, quality liability, a mature buying-house industry, and a possible registration requirement (BGBA) to check.
- **AI:** supplier research, specs and tech packs, Bangla↔English, document checks and reporting.

### M8 Bangla AI Data Desk (native-language edge)
- **We sell:** native Bangla work AI companies can't do well without humans: evaluation sets, red-teaming, reviewing model answers, speech and transcription checks, and cultural-safety review.
- **Who pays:** AI labs, data vendors (as their subcontractor team), and AI startups launching in South Asia.
- **Find/reach:** data-vendor vendor programs, AI-data job boards, research groups publishing Bangla benchmarks.
- **First step from home:** build a 50-item Bangla evaluation sample that shows a known model failing (proof from the outside).
- **Money:** per hour or per item; a team contract can be $1k+/month.
- **Path:** Home and Impressed are strong (we prove the model fails in Bangla). Trust is medium. Pay is via vendor platforms. Risk: gig-platform pricing ($14–24/h seen) and synthetic data replacing humans.
- **AI:** drafts candidate items; humans judge the native nuance (the part that is paid).

### M9 Human Sign-off Desk (verification as a service)
- **We sell:** a responsible human check on AI output before it goes public or into systems: AI-drafted documents, AI-extracted data, AI product content, AI chatbot answers. Sold per item with an error guarantee.
- **Who pays:** firms already using AI at volume that got burned by errors (chatbot liability cases exist: Air Canada 2024, a German appellate court in 2026).
- **Find/reach:** firms that publicly run AI (AI chat widgets, AI-written catalogs); test their AI from the outside and show errors.
- **First step from home:** test 30 public business chatbots with 10 standard questions each; log wrong answers with screenshots.
- **Money:** per check or monthly QA retainer; agencies that deploy AI bots can resell it (white-label).
- **Path:** Letter and Impressed are strong (we show their own bot lying). Reply is strong (liability fear). Trust is medium. Category is new (parity edge).
- **AI:** generates test questions and compares answers to the business's own public facts; humans confirm.

### M10 Platform-Native Seller (inbound by design)
- **We sell:** fixed-scope productized offers inside places buyers already search with intent and where the platform supplies trust and payment: Upwork Project Catalog, niche marketplaces, partner directories of fast-growing tools, data and dataset marketplaces.
- **Who pays:** buyers on the platform.
- **Find/reach:** the platform brings them (Read and Pay solved). Also answer posted requests with finished work attached.
- **First step from home:** pick one under-supplied listing category and publish 2 offers with real samples.
- **Money:** many small repeat buyers → reviews → higher price; agencies hire repeat.
- **Path:** Read, Trust and Pay are strong by design. Weak: a zero-review start and price competition, so pick categories where supply is thin.
- **AI:** delivers; the human handles QA and communication.

### M11 Recovery-for-Share (paid only from money recovered)
- **We sell:** finding and recovering money a business already lost (overcharges, refunds, reimbursements, unclaimed credits). The fee is a % of what is recovered, which counts as a halal reward for a result (ju'ala), not interest.
- **Who pays:** the business, from money it would not otherwise get.
- **Find/reach:** signals that a class of businesses is owed money (e.g. US importers and IEEPA refunds).
- **First step from home:** a public calculator or checklist showing "you may be owed $X".
- **Money:** 10–25% of recovered amounts; one recovery can be large.
- **Path:** Reply is very strong (free money) and Trust is helped (no upfront risk). Weak: needs their data or logins, collection risk, and possible licensing (customs brokers, attorneys).
- **AI:** document analysis and calculations; humans verify claims.

### M12 Benchmark Publisher (authority-first)
- **We sell:** nothing at first. We publish a public, data-backed study of an industry ("We tested 200 X; here's the scoreboard"). Named businesses and journalists react; we sell fixes, monitoring, custom reports or sponsorship.
- **Who pays:** the businesses in the study, vendors who serve that industry, and agencies.
- **Find/reach:** the study is the letter: "You're #143 of 200; here is why." People reply when named.
- **First step from home:** choose a measurable thing, collect data on 50–200 businesses and publish.
- **Money:** fixes + monitoring + paid annual report; authority compounds.
- **Path:** Read, Reply and Trust are strong by design (public, checkable evidence = credibility). Weak: needs careful accuracy and fairness (no shaming, honest methods).
- **AI:** data collection, analysis and writing; humans verify every data point.

### M13 Tool-Wrapped Service (service-as-software)
- **We sell:** a small AI tool + human check that delivers one outcome on subscription, one-to-many. AI writes the code.
- **Who pays:** many small buyers.
- **Find/reach:** needs distribution, which is the CEO's past failure (itch.io).
- **Note:** best used as the **scale stage** of a proven model, not a standalone start.
