# M3 — Sponsor-side desk: we run newsletter/podcast sponsorship buying for small brands that just started, and invoice the brand for our own service
Opus companion, 2026-10-30. Model M3, hunting ground 4 ("small brands opening a new channel"), built on the brief's third Pay mechanism: **"we invoice the sponsor for our own service"**. Open market (US, UK, Canada, Australia; B2B SaaS and DTC brands); no affinity variant, because the Muslim-sponsor pool is thin (see `M3-ramadan-sponsor-desk.md`).
**Labels:** VERIFIED · VENDOR CLAIM · SEARCH-SUMMARY · ESTIMATE · E3.

## What it is
Brands that have just started buying newsletter or podcast sponsorships are visible in public newsletter archives. We offer to plan their next 3 months of placements:
- find under-priced lists that fit them;
- negotiate;
- write the copy;
- tag every link;
- report cost per signup;
- cut losers.

The brand pays publishers directly; **we never hold media money**. We invoice a prepaid monthly fee, plus a bonus on tracked signups if they share the figure. That is the M3 "commission on revenue we can see" in its cleanest legal form.

## (c) Filters
- **Work level:** medium-high.
- **Demand trend, dated:** marketer adoption of newsletters at 64% in 2025 (+25% YoY) and Paved campaigns +40% (22 Jan 2026 report). More brands are buying; but the buying tools are growing as fast (beehiiv doubled its ad team, 8 Jan 2026).
- **AI substitution:** medium-high. List discovery and reporting are tool features; the human part is judgement and copy.
- **Team fit:** medium. Research is the CEO's strength. Zero media-buying record is the gap.
- **Halal:** client and placement screening is needed. A fee for a service is fine.

## (h) Red team
- **Strongest reason it fails:** the brand's ad buyer is the most-pitched person in this market. Book a Sponsor alone sent 39,151 pitches in 30 days (VENDOR CLAIM), and Paved and Passionfroot already offer managed buying with track records. Brands at the "just started" stage spend $1–5k a month and don't hire an outside buyer for that. We have bought no media, so our advice is untested.
- **Counter-evidence search run:** "newsletter sponsorship agency for brands management fee… minimum budget" found only generic ad-agency fee ranges and platform fees, no small specialist buyers with public prices. Demand for an outside buyer at this spend level is unproven.

I'd keep it only as a stage-2 service for owners or brands met through the product-partner route, not as a cold-outreach business.
