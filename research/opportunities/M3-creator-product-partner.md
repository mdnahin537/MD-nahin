# M3 — Creator product partner: build a digital product line with an audience owner, paid by the platform's automatic split
Opus companion, 2026-10-30. Model M3. This is new ground, not in models.md's list. It is the only M3 road where **Pay works by mechanism**: Gumroad itself pays our share into a Bangladeshi bank, so the owner never has to pay us.

Two variants are scored side by side, per the CEO's 2026-10-30 scope rule:
- **A (affinity):** English-language Muslim creators, with a Ramadan 1448 launch.
- **O (open market):** teaching, parenting and homeschool creators in the UK, US, Canada, Australia, Ireland and New Zealand.

**Labels:** VERIFIED · VENDOR CLAIM · SEARCH-SUMMARY · ESTIMATE · E3.

## What it is
We find owners with an engaged audience and no product (or only donations). From their own public content we make a small paid product: a Ramadan reflection journal built from their 30 episodes, a kids' activity pack using their show's stories, or a teacher's planning kit from their most-asked questions. They list it on Gumroad and add us as a **collaborator** at a fixed cut (≤ 50%). Gumroad pays each side automatically at the time of sale. Alternative split: we list the product and they earn through Gumroad's affiliate split.

The owner pays nothing, holds no money of ours, and approves every word.

**Why this is M3 and not the killed "course production":**
- We charge **no production fee**.
- We are paid **only by a platform-tracked share of new revenue**.
- The products are small downloadable assets, not video courses.
- The buyer is the audience owner, who sells it.

The killed idea was selling production as a service. This is a revenue partnership with a different payment path. G6 is passed conditionally; the critic should check this point.

## Evidence base

- **Gumroad collaborators** (VERIFIED, gumroad.com/help/article/341-collaborations, re-opened by me): "the collaborator's portion is added to their Gumroad balance and is paid out the same way as all other Gumroad creators and affiliates"; "the maximum cut is 50%"; collaborators must have payment settings filled in; adding a collaborator removes the product's existing affiliates.

Gumroad does "not support… Payoneer, Wise… wire transfer" (helper 1, official "Getting paid" page opened and re-opened; consistent with the M7 finding). Gumroad's own fees are not re-verified here; a commonly cited figure is 10% + $0.50 per direct sale (SEARCH-SUMMARY).

The money these owners leave on the table is their audience's willingness to buy, and the M7 lesson ("sales go to creators with audiences") says that is where digital products actually sell.

## (e) Money path and 3 scenarios
**Path:** first product for one owner → launch sales, our share $100–300 (ESTIMATE) → 4–6 owners × an annual Ramadan relaunch plus 1 evergreen product each → $1,000+/month in season, a tail off-season.

**Formula:** owners contacted × yes × launched × (audience reached × conversion × price) × our share × (1 + tail).

| Scenario | Assumptions (ESTIMATE) | Result per year |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 40 owners × 10% yes × 33% launch = 1.3 products × $400 gross × 40% | **≈ $210** |
| Mid | 60 × 15% × 50% = 4.5 products × $1,500 gross × 40% × 1.3 tail | **≈ $3,500 (~$290/month)** |
| High | 86 + 40 (O) × 20% × 60% = 15 products × $3,000 × 45% × 1.5 | **≈ $30,000 (~$2,500/month)** |

The $1,500 gross in the mid case = 10,000 listeners reached × 1% × $15. Gumroad's fee comes off the gross (SEARCH-SUMMARY ~10% + $0.50/sale) before the split. Payouts below $100 roll over (VERIFIED minimum).

## (h) Red team
- **Strongest reason it fails:**
  - Small creators' audiences don't buy enough. 10,000 downloads is not 10,000 buyers, and a 1% conversion is an ESTIMATE with no evidence for this niche.
  - The products themselves are commodities ($0–5 on Gumroad).
  - Rev-share vendors already pitch creators.
  - Gumroad, our only verified rail, is the platform losing share (GMV down 2021→2023).
  - Owners on Stan or Etsy may refuse a second store.
- **Counter-evidence searches run:**
  1. "rev share… build digital product… for my audience": found only vendors offering it, not owners asking for it. So there is no posted need: Read stays 1.
  2. Gumroad Ramadan planner pages: $0–5 with 0–1 ratings, i.e. no sales signal.
  3. Platform GMV trend: Gumroad down, Whop up. Whop's payout rail to Bangladesh is NOT checked; it is a PARTNER CHECK before any Whop variant.

## (i) vs Shothik
- **Read: slightly better.** An email published in their own feed, vs Facebook requests. Both are unproven on "noticed".
- **Trust: better.** The proof is their own content turned into a product. Risk is zero for them, and no login is needed.
- **Pay: clearly better.** The platform pays both sides at the moment of sale. No Zelle or cash problem and no collection.

## (j) Intelligence verdict
This is the only M3 road where the formula's weak step (Pay) is solved by mechanism, not by trust. It is VERIFIED at Gumroad's own help pages and fits the CEO's existing Gumroad know-how. What the formula can't see is volume: I have no evidence of what an owner-branded Muslim product sells. The test is cheap and dated (Ramadan relaunch every year). I'd run A before O, and judge it on one number: products launched by 15 January 2027. If it works, M3's other routes (sponsor intros) become stage-2 add-ons for the same owners.
