## (h) Red team
**Strongest reason it fails:** platforms hire individuals directly at scale, run their own ID checks (TELUS: "complete ID verification", VERIFIED), and set the price. A two-person pod adds a layer they don't pay for. The referral share is capped near one week's pay, so the pod earns a bounty, not a business.
**Counter-evidence search I ran:** "AI data annotation platform contributor fraud account sharing…" → AlgorithmWatch (2025-05-22, VERIFIED) shows platforms are "losing control of their supply chain" to account trading. That *supports* verification, but the same article says platforms do KYC themselves through phone, address, ID and tax checks, so they solve it in-house. Search "become a vendor … Appen OR LXT OR Defined.ai OR Sigma.ai OR Welocalize" → no vendor-onboarding page found (NOT FOUND). **The vendor route is unproven.**
