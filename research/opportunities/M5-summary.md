## The central findings (read first)
1. **The biggest dated wave reachable from Dhaka is Gulf e-invoicing, not a UK or US rule.**
   - **UAE:** every business making B2B/B2G sales, VAT-registered or not, must appoint an Accredited Service Provider by **31 Mar 2027** and e-invoice from **1 Jul 2027**.
   - **Fines:** AED 5,000 a month for no ASP, and AED 100 per bad invoice, capped at 5,000 a month.
   - **Sources:** KPMG 22 Jul 2026 and BDO, both opened. Only the large-firm ASP deadline moved, once.
   - **Neighbours:** Saudi Wave 25 (revenue above SAR 187,500) must integrate by **1 Feb 2027** (ZATCA, VERIFIED). Oman's two bands start **1 Apr / 1 Oct 2027** (PRESS).
   - **Fit:** UAE office hours are Dhaka daytime.
2. **The UAE regulator publishes the middlemen with named contacts.**
   - The MoF page lists **80 accredited ASPs**, each with accreditation number, contact person, email and phone (VERIFIED, "Last updated on 29/10/2026"; E3 count).
   - On 12 Jun 2026 there were 41 pre-approved providers (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
   - That is M5's missing piece: a public list of middlemen for one wave.
3. **The UK's best wave is a records rule with money attached.**
   - Under packaging EPR, a pack is **"reported as red if you do not have evidence"** that it is amber or green. Evidence must be kept for 7 years (GOV.UK RAM 2027 guidance, VERIFIED).
   - The red multiplier is 1.2× now → 1.6× → 2.0× (VENDOR CLAIM, ERP UK).
   - The next report is due **1 Apr 2027**.
   - **Catch:** Valpak (2,753 members) already sells automated RAM scoring and supplier chasing (VERIFIED).
4. **Most of the waves the manager listed fail F1–F5** (see the table below). LDC graduation may move: Bangladesh requested a deferral to 2029, and a draft UN General Assembly resolution was reported tabled on 29 Oct 2026 (SEARCH-SUMMARY). EUDR and the Companies House accounts reform have each moved twice.
