# M6 Dhaka Ground Desk — Phase 2 summary
Opus companion, 2026-10-29. Helper runs used: **2 of 5**, both Sonnet. Helper 1 (effort high) built 34 foreign screening firms with Bangladesh prices and gaps. ## The central findings (read these first)
1. **The model's thesis ("only people in Bangladesh can do this") is mostly false for a desk-only team.** It holds for one narrow job: making Bangladeshi universities and boards answer foreign verification requests (local fee rails, Bangla calls to the right office, the occasional Dhaka errand).
   - Employment calls are being automated: AI voice agents already sell US employment verification to screening firms (Superunit claims 70,000+, VENDOR CLAIM, YC page opened).
   - Bank-statement checks were made self-service by Bangladesh Bank's QR mandate of 12 May 2026 (PRESS, Bangladesh Monitor, opened).
   - Company checks are already sold by Dhaka vendors and by the US Commercial Service.
2. **A Bangladeshi university named the buyers.** Dhaka University's 2026 statement (Daily Star; UHR request dated 2026-03-04) says verification requests come only through intermediaries: "WES, First Advantage, Qualification Check, DataFlow, AMS India, and UHR". It charges **USD 50 per student**, paid by the intermediary or at Sonali Bank's DU branch, and unpaid requests stall (PRESS, two articles opened).
3. **UK universities pay intermediaries to verify, and price "high-risk" countries higher.**
   - Manchester uses Qualification Check where no online system exists, adds checks for UKVI higher-risk countries, and pays the fee itself (VERIFIED).
   - Ecctis charges from £50 per verification **+£20 for high-risk countries** (VERIFIED).
   - The FT/Qualification Check analysis of ~55,000 offers puts Bangladesh third for falsified qualifications (PRESS, bdnews24 2026-06-28).
   - **Qualification Check's own 2025/26 report** (17,663 checks for 40 UK universities): checks on Bangladeshi qualifications **"more than tripled"**, and Bangladesh's fraud signal rose from 2.6% to 9.5% (VENDOR data, report page opened). This is the strongest dated demand signal in M6, from a buyer's own data.
4. **Supply is already crowded.**
   - Dhaka has at least 8 screening vendors (HRBD BDT 10,999–42,999 per candidate; Station Dhaka Quick Verify **$800**; Enroute, 17 years; VENDOR CLAIMS, pages opened).
   - Foreign firms publish Bangladesh checks: CRS Checks $69 per education or employment check, 15–25 days; Verifile 5–15 working days; Zinc reports a 3-day average for Bangladesh education (helper 1, VERIFIED pages).
   - Background Check Group already sells a **University of Dhaka degree check for $49** (+ university charges, 10–15 working days) (VERIFIED). First Advantage lists Bangladesh among its office locations (VERIFIED).
   - The gap is speed, the hard institutions (DU, NU) and local fee payment, not coverage. ClearStar says global screeners use local suppliers because fees must be paid "in local currency", in advance or by bank draft (VERIFIED, 2017 blog).
5. **Planes cleared or quantified.**
   - **Data law:** the Personal Data Protection Act 2026 (Act 63, dated 10 Apr 2026) is in force from 6 Nov 2025. Its penalty sections (31–35) start only on a gazetted date at least 18 months after issue. Consent must be specific, revocable and informed about transfers; processors act under the controller's contract (VERIFIED, bdlaws).
   - **Licences:** no private-enquiry licence regime exists (PRESS, TBS). Buying-house DoT registration applies only if we place orders, which we won't (PRESS, FE).
   - **Insurance:** PBSA's General Standard asks accredited firms for $1M E&O, or an attestation where cover is not commonly available (VERIFIED). Whether this flows down to suppliers is NOT FOUND.
   - **Informal payments:** a real risk. TIB's 2023 survey found 50.8% of households paid bribes, and 77.2% of payers said the service "cannot be obtained" otherwise (VERIFIED). Refuse, and accept slower cases.

## My bet: #1, as a 7-day test, with #2 and #3 as bundled second products for the same clients
**Why:**
- It is the one M6 road where "only in Bangladesh" holds for a desk team: local fee rails (DU's $50 fee, payable at Sonali Bank), Bangla calls to the right registrar desk, and a weekly Dhaka errand.
- A Bangladeshi university's own statement names the buyers and the failure, which makes the letter and the proof.
- Trust is designed in: the institution emails the intermediary directly, so the buyer never has to trust our word. This is the only design that survives the insider-fraud objection raised in the FT/Qualification Check analysis.
- Pay is B2B to Payoneer (UK GBP transfer free; US ACH 1%).
**What would kill it:**
- The 7-day calls show most institutions already answer foreign requests fast once paid. Zinc's 3-day Bangladesh average is the warning sign.
- The first replies say "we already have a Bangladesh route". First Advantage lists a Bangladesh location; Back Check sells DU/NU checks at $49.
**Realistic ceiling:** $500–2,000/month from liaison plus bundled employment and company checks. It grows only with a trained Dhaka helper and 2–3 steady intermediaries.
