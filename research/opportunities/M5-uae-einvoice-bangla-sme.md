## The core evidence
1. **The pool exists and grew.**
   - Dubai Chambers (26 Feb 2025, VERIFIED primary): **8,686 Bangladeshi companies** were active Dubai Chamber members at the end of 2024, "annual growth of 19%".
   - Financial Express (27 Sep 2023, PRESS, opened): 1,044 Bangladeshi firms joined in H1 2023, against 711 in H1 2022 (+47%).
2. **They are in scope even without VAT** (PRESS, BDO, opened). The system applies to B2B and B2G "whether or not the transacting party is registered for VAT"; non-VAT businesses need a TIN. B2C (retail shops, restaurants selling to the public) is out.
3. **The cost of the ASP itself can be zero:** the first 100 e-invoices a year are free (VENDOR CLAIM, BusinessDubai; SEARCH-SUMMARY, Decision 64/2025). That cuts both their pain and their budget.
4. **No Bangladeshi middleman was found.**
   - I found no ICAB (Bangladesh) chapter in the UAE.
   - ICAI Dubai is the largest of the Indian institute's 53 overseas chapters, and ICAP UAE honoured 72 Pakistani chartered accountants (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
   - A "Bangladesh Business Council, Dubai" was formed around 2011 with 60 members (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Gulf News, undated) and later signed an MoU with FBCCI (undated).
   - Its current membership and contact: NOT FOUND.
