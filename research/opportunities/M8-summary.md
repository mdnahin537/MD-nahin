## The central findings (read first)
1. **M8's money is not in restaurants. It is in faith charities, reached through their accountants.**
   - The Charity Commission's public data carries an HMRC Gift Aid flag. Only **18%** of mosques with £25–100k income and **37%** with £100–500k are recognised for Gift Aid, against 46% and 56% for all charities (my count, E3).
   - Of 25 corporate Bangladeshi-linked mosques whose accounts we opened, **20 show no Gift Aid line**.
   - **One East London accountant (AM Accountancy Services) examines 4 of them**: ≈ £570k of donations a year, with no Gift Aid among them.
   - HMRC rules allow a **4-year backlog** and declarations covering the 4 prior years. Small-donation (GASDS) cash from 2024/25 expires **5 April 2027** (all VERIFIED, HMRC guidance updated 2026-10-20).
2. **The licence plane is avoidable by design:**
   - Only an authorised official (a trustee, official, employee or manager) or a notified nominee can sign claims.
   - Mandatory tax-adviser registration (from 18 May 2026) applies only to advisers who *interact with HMRC on behalf of clients*.
   - So we prepare and the mosque submits (VERIFIED).
3. **Two hidden planes the formula would miss:**
   - **Fee form.** Hanafi answers object to collectors taking a % of donations (SEARCH-SUMMARY). Fixed fees only, never paid from zakat.
   - **PECR.** Unincorporated charities are "individual subscribers" (ICO, VERIFIED), so ~2/3 of target mosques can't be cold-emailed. The examiner accountant is the door.
4. **The register flag can be stale.** Chester Shah Jalal is flagged "not recognised" but its accounts show £6,431 of Gift Aid. Letters must quote the accounts.
5. **Shothik, re-scored:**
   - **Road is fixed:** NYC open data lists **79 "Bangladeshi" + 312 "Indian"** restaurants with phones (E3).
   - **Still weak by design:** Read (phone only, Dhaka night), Reply (no trigger), Trust-risk (logins) and Pay (Zelle/cash habit) still fail. G5 can't be shown.
   - **It can't beat its weak steps as specified.** Only a tax-preparer resale (untested) or moving to the UK changes that.
6. **RTW wave is real but the product isn't.**
   - Right-to-work checks were extended to workers, subcontractors and gig riders from **1 Oct 2026** (gov.uk, VERIFIED).
   - The Jan–Mar 2026 penalty register names **≥ 20 South-Asian restaurants and takeaways** at £40k–180k each (E3).
   - But no case proof is possible from outside, staff passports would have to leave the UK, and licensed premises fail the halal filter.

## Three most important verified facts
1. **HMRC guidance (updated 2026-10-20):**
   - Claims are repaid only if signed by an authorised official (trustee/official/employee/manager) or a notified nominee.
   - Ultimate responsibility stays with the charity.
   - The time limit is 4 years.
   - A declaration can cover "all donations I have made for the 4 years prior to this tax year".
   - GASDS must be claimed within 2 years.
   - Tax-adviser registration applies to those who interact with HMRC on behalf of clients.
2. **Charity Commission data (field `charity_gift_aid`) + filed accounts:**
   - Mosques' Gift Aid recognition runs well below all charities.
   - 20 of 25 Bangladeshi-linked corporate mosques show no Gift Aid line.
   - Brick Lane Jamme Masjid claimed £18,572 (2024), then £2,950 (2025).
   - Swiftaid charges 5% + VAT (claims) and 10% + VAT (missed Gift Aid found).
3. **RTW:** checks extended from 1 Oct 2026 (gov.uk guide and code finalised 2026-10-01). Penalties are £45k first / £60k repeat per worker. The Jan–Mar 2026 penalty register (published 2026-08-27) lists 291 entries, ≥ 20 of them South-Asian restaurants and takeaways.
