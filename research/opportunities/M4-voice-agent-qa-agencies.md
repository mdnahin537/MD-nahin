## (h) Red team
**Strongest reason it fails:**
- The buyer can get the same thing free or for cents, inside the platform it already uses (Retell 2025, Vapi 2026) or from a funded startup (Coval's free audit).
- Human "independent QA" offers in the very community where these buyers gather got no buyer replies across six posts in Aug–Oct 2026. The only response was one Diamond partner's comment that integration QA is "underserved" (2026-10-07). That is mild counter-evidence: a builder sees the gap, but nobody paid in public.
- MARUPA pitched "outcome" QA (did the CRM or calendar really update?) on 2026-08-24 to three separate buyer threads and posted its own offer. No visible takers.

**Counter-evidence search (for the idea):** looked for agencies *buying* human QA. Found only:
- one US-only Upwork post, "tester for voice agent used in real estate acquisitions" (SEARCH-SUMMARY, relative date);
- low-rate generic testers ($4–20/h).

Buyer posts in the Retell forum ask for **builders and fixers**, not testers. The idea's assumption, "small agencies buy QA from people", is **not supported** by what I found.
