# M8 — Bangladesh end-use monitoring desk for UK diaspora charities
**Model:** M8 Affinity Desk (with M6 Dhaka-presence) · **Companion:** Phase 2 Opus, 2026-10-29
**One line:** Hundreds of small UK charities run by British Bangladeshis send money to projects in Bangladesh: orphan sponsorship, madrasas, wells, Qurbani meat, flood relief. The Charity Commission expects trustees to carry out *and document* partner due diligence and end-use monitoring, and it has criticised trustees for missing records **even when no money was misused**. We produce the trustees' file from Dhaka, desk-only:
- a know-your-partner check (NGO Affairs Bureau registration, bank account in the partner's name, people);
- a remote monitoring report each campaign: Bangla video calls with the partner and a random sample of beneficiaries, receipts matched to transfers, geotagged photo rules;
- an English summary mapped to the Commission's checklist.
Paid per report by UK bank transfer.
**Labels:** VERIFIED · E3 · VENDOR CLAIM · SEARCH-SUMMARY · ESTIMATE · NOT FOUND.

## (h) Red team
**Strongest reason it fails:** these charities' trustees *are* the monitoring. They fly to Sylhet, their relatives check the wells, and they would see an outside reporter as an accusation. The ones with real problems will never hire someone to document them.
- Substitute: relatives and the local partner's own photos and videos, free.
- Our report could also surface an unregistered partner. Under the Foreign Donations (Voluntary Activities) Regulation Act 2016, organisations receiving foreign donations must register with the NGO Affairs Bureau, and banks may not release funds without Bureau approval (ICNL English text, SEARCH-SUMMARY). That puts us in an awkward, even risky, position in Bangladesh.

**Counter-evidence searches run:**
1. Commission inquiries into Bangladesh-linked charities 2025–26: **none found**. The latest found is 2023. The regulatory push is steady, not surging.
2. Monitoring-visit price for small charities: **NOT FOUND**. The paid market may not exist below large INGOs.
