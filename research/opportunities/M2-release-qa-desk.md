# M2 — Release QA desk: a named human tests and signs off each release for small software teams that post QA-tester roles
**Model:** M2 Replacement Desk (Mode A first, Mode B to scale) · **Companion:** Phase 2 Opus, 2026-10-29
**One line:** Small software teams post "manual QA / tester" roles on Upwork and remote boards. We answer with 2–3 real bugs we found on *their own live app*, each with a 20-second video. Then we run their release checks as a managed role. AI drafts the test plan and bug reports; a named human runs every check on real browsers and phones and signs the release sheet. To scale, we add testers we have met in person in Dhaka (Mode B), working under the partner's checklist.
**Labels:** VERIFIED = primary page or API data opened by me on 2026-10-29 · VENDOR CLAIM · SEARCH-SUMMARY (page not opened) · ESTIMATE · NOT FOUND.
**Why this role family:** it passes the M4 lesson better than the others I tried. (1) The paid part is human confidence in shipping AI-written code, and Upwork's own data shows QA demand **rising** while AI spreads. (2) The proof is self-checkable on the buyer's own app with no login. (3) The partner's exact, checklist-driven skill *is* the product. Its main threat is AI testers built into app builders (G4).

## (c) Filters
- **Work level:** medium-high. Test charters, cross-browser and device passes, reproducible bug reports, regression sheets, release sign-off. Not a 10-minute task.
- **Demand trend:** see G5 (four dated points, 2025-01 → 2026-10).
- **AI-substitution risk (12–24 months):** medium. Builders test their own code; the human sign-off on real devices and judgment of "does this make sense to a user" is what is left, and it is what Upwork's buyers say they lack confidence in.
- **Team fit:** the best in M2. The partner's exact checklist work *is* the product. The CEO drafts AI test charters and handles buyers. Fully async: bugs and reports are written. No calls needed.
- **Halal:** screen out betting, interest-banking and crypto-yield products (G1).

## (g) Monday walk-through
1. **Partner:** start the Upwork post count (asset: the new Upwork account ✔; filters are free).
2. **CEO:** pick 3 public apps from live posts; have AI draft a 15-step test charter for each; run one pass with a free screen recorder (assets: laptop, phone, Claude ✔).
3. **Partner:** write the release sign-off checklist and the bug-report template (asset: Google Docs ✔).
**Planes:** (i) Connects cost money: ~$10 this month, justified by the test. (ii) Testing iOS Safari needs an iPhone; if neither founder has one, start with Android plus desktop browsers and say so in proposals. (iii) The Upwork profile has no reviews: offset by the bug video.

## (h) Red team
**Strongest reason it fails:** QA posts draw dozens of cheap testers from South Asia, so Reply may sit near zero even with a bug video. Meanwhile the vibe-coding builders, the fastest-growing source of new small apps, now test themselves (Replit Agent 3, VERIFIED).
**Counter-evidence searches I ran:** (1) "vibe coded apps … QA testers demand 2026" → mostly testing-tool vendors claiming demand (self-interested, SEARCH-SUMMARY). (2) "Replit Agent 3 app testing…" → built-in browser self-testing since Sept 2025 (VERIFIED). (3) Upwork's monthly reports → QA demand *up* in Sept and Nov 2025 (VERIFIED), which cuts against substitution for now. Net: real demand, real substitution pressure; the PARTNER CHECK on proposal counts decides whether Reply can work.

## (i) vs Shothik
- **Read: better.** The buyer posted the job and reads proposals (platform), vs a Facebook message request.
- **Trust: better.** Proof on their own app plus Upwork escrow, vs a stranger asking a restaurant for Google access.
- **Pay: better.** Upwork escrow to a Bangladesh bank, vs a restaurant's Zelle or cash.
- Weakest point: worse on **Reply** (crowded bidding) than Shothik's uncontested niche.

## (j) Intelligence verdict
The formula gives this 3 of 3 because Upwork carries Read, Trust and Pay. The real fights are Reply (crowding) and a 12–24-month AI window. I still rank it the best M2 *start*: the proof is honest and self-checkable, no logins are needed before value is shown, the partner's precision is the product, and Upwork's own 2025 data says buyers are paying humans to check AI-made software. The business is not the founders testing forever. It is a sign-off desk that places in-person-verified Dhaka testers, which also builds the references that M2's other routes lack.
