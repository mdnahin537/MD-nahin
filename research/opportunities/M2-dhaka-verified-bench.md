# M2 — Dhaka Verified Bench: in-person-verified Bangladeshi candidates for intermediaries that already post Bangladesh-open roles
**Model:** M2 Replacement Desk (Mode B, sold to the middleman: route Ra) · **Companion:** Phase 2 Opus, 2026-10-29 · **Helper:** helper 1 (Sonnet), checked by me
**One line:** We keep a bench of Bangladeshi professionals we have met in person in Dhaka, with ID and degrees checked against originals, references called in Bangla, and a filmed task in their field. We place them into roles that intermediaries have already posted. **First money (no sales pitch needed):** the public referral programmes of AI-talent platforms that post Bangladesh-explicit roles. micro1 pays a flat amount per role, "up to $3,000", after the candidate works 10 hours; Mercor pays 20% of earnings up to a cap. **Second money:** sourcing deals with offshore staffing agencies, benchmarked at a $2,499 one-time sourcing fee or $150–200/month per managed worker.
**Labels:** VERIFIED = primary page or API data opened by me or by helper 1 and spot-checked by me on 2026-10-29 · VENDOR CLAIM · SEARCH-SUMMARY (page not opened) · ESTIMATE · NOT FOUND.

**Why the middleman, not the end employer:** in the fraud wave, an end employer distrusts an unknown offshore agency (see `M2-direct-verified-hire.md`). An intermediary already sells remote talent, already carries the fraud risk, and **already publishes what it pays for outside help**.
**What helper 1 changed (important):** most "worldwide" staffing agencies in my seed list actually recruit only in **Latin America, the Philippines, South Africa or Nigeria**. GoFasti, TalentCross, Talent Hackers, Job Duck, Coconut, KDCI and NightOwl all say so on their own sites or posts (VERIFIED). Only a handful post roles that *explicitly* list Bangladesh: TechBiz Global (9 posts), Wing Assistant (BD/IN/PH), micro1, Invisible, Mercor, Xperteez and Devop.One (VERIFIED, Himalayas API). So the staffing-agency route means persuading most of them to *add* a country, which is a weak Read. The AI-talent platforms already want Bangladeshis and pay referrers, so they are the first buyer.

## (e) Money path and 3-scenario estimate
**Path:** referral payouts from AI-talent platforms (first $100–300 within weeks of the first verified candidate) → with 3–5 checkable placements, sourcing deals with 2–3 agencies at $300–500 per hire (well under Vitalis's $2,499) → per-seat "verified and managed" fee of $50–150/month for placed people (under Vitalis's $150–200 benchmark).
Formula (referral route): candidates verified/month × pass rate × average payout. Formula (agency route): agencies × hires/month × fee + seats × monthly fee.

## (h) Red team
**Strongest reason it fails:** referral programmes are the platform's cheapest acquisition channel, and platforms change them at will. Mercor already caps the share at 4× the first hourly rate (a third-party tracker dates a "referral bonus cap" change to 2026-07-29, SEARCH-SUMMARY). micro1 pays once per person. So the referral route is bounty income that can shrink overnight. The agency route is weaker than it looked, because most offshore agencies have chosen other countries.
**Counter-evidence searches I ran (and helper 1 ran):** (1) do agencies exclude Bangladesh? GoFasti "Hire top talent in Latin America", TalentCross "professionals from Latin America", Talent Hackers (Nigeria/South America), Allshore "EMEA Only" (VERIFIED by helper 1): exclusion by region, none names Bangladesh. (2) Do agencies refuse outside recruiters? NOT FOUND among intermediaries. (3) Do they pay outside partners? Yes, mostly as client-referral bounties (Vitalis $500, Radar Hire $600/year per position) and candidate-referral bounties (micro1); a per-hire *sourcing* fee to outside suppliers was found only at Hyring (VERIFIED).

## (i) vs Shothik
- **Read: better** on the referral route (the platform asked publicly; no inbox to break into); **same** on the agency route.
- **Trust: better.** The platform runs identity and skill checks; we add in-person proof. Shothik needed Google access.
- **Pay: mixed.** Mercor's bank transfer is better than a restaurant's Zelle; micro1's rail is unknown.

## (j) Intelligence verdict
The formula rewards the referral route with a two-key pass, correctly: the platform has already solved Read and Trust and pays on results. But it is bounty income on someone else's policy, and it only becomes a business if the bench feeds a second, owned revenue: per-seat "verified and managed" fees and agency sourcing deals, which need references first. **I'd run it as M2's Mode B engine behind the QA desk**, not as the lead. Its best use is to turn "met in person in Dhaka" from a claim into a track record.
