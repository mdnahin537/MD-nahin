## The central finding (read this first)
The model's named first product, human QA of AI chat/voice agents for small agencies, has **already lost its window**:
- Retell built simulation and batch testing in on 2025-03-14 and launched automated QA ("Assure") for 2026-01-01.
- Vapi launched Simulations on 2026-08-18.
- Coval gives a **free** 30-simulation audit.
- Cekura charges $0.25 per test minute.

In the Retell community, six "independent QA / audit" offers posted between Aug and Oct 2026 drew **no buyer replies** (12–31 views each; 5 got none, 1 got a single partner comment calling integration QA "underserved"). Buyers there ask for builders and fixers, not testers. All of these are primary pages I opened.

M4 survives only where the paid human part is something software *and* cheap labour cannot supply. For this team that is **native Bangla, with Sylheti through a recruited panel**.

## My bet: #1, the language panel, as a 7-day, ≤$5 test, not a commitment
**Why:**
- It is the only M4 road where the paid part cannot be done by software (no Sylheti TTS/ASR in market) or by cheaper offshore testers (the skill is the language, not the labour).
- The trigger is sharp and dated: three EMMA withdrawals or accent stories in five weeks, Healthwatch England reporting to DHSC, an ICB note on "language barriers", and a funded vendor claiming "85+ languages" with no public test.
- The proof uses the vendor's own public demo line, with no login. InTouchNow and Wavenet print the same demo number, so one test covers two buyers.
- Pay is the buyer's normal rail: UK bank transfer via Payoneer GBP, free.

**What would kill it:**
- The demo lines handle Bangla well. Then the letter loses its bite: pivot to "native-speaker evidence for selling into East London" or drop it.
- No Sylheti speaker can be recruited. That is a CEO question.

**Ceiling:** an honest $300–2,500/month niche. It grows only by adding verified native-speaker varieties to the panel and new buyers (AI-scribe vendors, Ufonia's 10-language trial).
