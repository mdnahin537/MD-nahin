**Model:** M8 Affinity Desk (with the M5 recovery mechanism) · **Companion:** Phase 2 Opus, 2026-10-29
**One line:** Many UK mosques take six-figure donations a year but are **not recognised by HMRC for Gift Aid**, so they reclaim nothing. We prepare everything for the mosque's own authorised official to submit: the HMRC recognition application, bilingual (Bangla/English) donor declaration forms and envelopes, a declaration drive to regular donors that covers up to 4 past years, the claim schedule, and the Small Donations Scheme cash records. Sold first to Bangladeshi-run mosques, and through the accountants who examine their accounts and the councils of mosques. Fixed fees only, paid after delivery or after HMRC pays.

## The core evidence (read first)
1. **The register shows the gap, in public.** The Charity Commission's public data has a field `charity_gift_aid`: "whether the charity is registered for gift aid with HMRC" (data definition, VERIFIED). The register page shows it as, for example, "Gift aid: Not recognised by HMRC for gift aid" (Burdett Bangladeshi Cultural Association's register page, VERIFIED).
2. **Mosques lag other charities (my count, E3, extract dated 2026-10-29).** Among registered charities named mosque/masjid/jamia/jame (n = 491):
   | Income band | Mosques recognised for Gift Aid | All E&W charities recognised |
   |---|---|---|
   | £25k–100k | 24 of 136 = **18%** | 46% |
   | £100k–500k | 71 of 194 = **37%** | 56% |
   | £500k+ | 36 of 46 = 78% | 65% |

   - **228 mosque-named charities with income ≥ £25k are not recognised.** Their latest income totals £46.7m.
   - Among all 1,656 Islamic-named charities, **596 with income ≥ £25k** are not recognised.
   - **86 Bangladeshi-linked mosques and Islamic centres with income ≥ £30k are not recognised** (by name cue or a Tower Hamlets/Newham postcode). 27 of them are CIOs or companies.
3. **Their own accounts confirm it (E3).** I opened 3 sets of accounts and helper 1 opened 25 (`research/handoffs/M8-helper-1-mosques.md`). Of the 25 corporate candidates, **20 show no Gift Aid line**, and only 1 shows a clear Gift Aid amount (Chester, £6,431; its register flag is stale). Four accountancy firms each examine 2–4 of these mosques. AM Accountancy Services (E1) examines 4, with ≈ £570k of donations a year and no Gift Aid among them. Examples:
   - **Burdett Bangladeshi Cultural Association and Mosque** (E14, CIO 1189772), year to 31 Jul 2025:
     - donations £185,651, plus £105,224 for the freehold mosque, ≈ **£290,875**;
     - **no Gift Aid line**;
     - £2,529 of card-machine charges, so identifiable card donors exist;
     - examined by AM Accountancy Services, E1.
   - **Darul Hadis Latifiah Foundation** (E2, CIO 1200935), year to 31 Aug 2024:
     - donations £136,113 (2023: £114,110), **no Gift Aid line**;
     - most income is madrasa fees (£401k), which can never carry Gift Aid;
     - filing 121 days overdue;
     - examiner: iTax Solutions Ltd, Southend.
   - **Calibration: Brick Lane Jamme Masjid** (E1, recognised for Gift Aid):
     - 2024: Gift Aid refund **£18,572 on £196,464** of donations and collections (9.5%);
     - 2025: only **£2,950 on £224,644** (1.3%), which looks like a missed or late claim;
     - accountancy and examination fees £8,701 a year.
   - So a Bangladeshi-run East London mosque that claims gets about 9.5% of its donations back. Even a recognised mosque can let its claims lapse.
4. **The rules allow a backlog and fix the dates (VERIFIED, HMRC Charities guidance, chapters 3 and 6, updated 2026-10-20; Small Donations Scheme guidance):**
   - **Back years:** claims may be made up to **4 years** after the end of the tax year (trusts) or accounting period (other charities) (6.5.1).
   - **Declarations reach back:** a declaration may cover "all donations I have made for the 4 years prior to this tax year" (chapter 3). Regular, identifiable donors (standing orders, card, bank transfer) can therefore be Gift-Aided backwards.
   - **Small Donations Scheme (GASDS):** a top-up on cash and contactless gifts of £30 or less, up to £8,000 of gifts (£2,000 of top-up) a year, more where the charity has community buildings.
     - It needs a Gift Aid claim in the same tax year, and the **matching rule** caps it at 10 × the Gift Aid donations.
     - It must be claimed **within 2 years** of the end of the tax year. Cash collected in 2024/25 expires **5 April 2027**.
5. **The licence plane is avoidable by design (VERIFIED):**
   - HMRC repays only claims signed by an **authorised official** (a trustee, official, employee or other manager of the charity), or by a nominee or collection agency notified on ChA1/ChV1 (6.3.1, 6.3.4).
   - "Ultimate responsibility… will remain with the charity" (6.3.8).
   - Mandatory tax-adviser registration (Finance Act 2026; staged from 18 May 2026) applies to advisers "who interact with HMRC on behalf of their clients" (gov.uk policy paper, 2025-11-26).
   - **Design rule:** we prepare files and the authorised official submits. We never log in to HMRC, never phone HMRC, and never become a nominee.

## (h) Red team
**Strongest honest reason it fails:**
- Many mosques are not registered **on purpose**. Committees may distrust HMRC contact, avoid the paperwork, or fear scrutiny of cash handling. They will read a stranger's email about "HMRC refunds" as a scam; Gift Aid fraud cases make trustees wary (several convictions 2013–2018, SEARCH-SUMMARY).
- **The recoverable base may be thin:**
  - most cash in boxes has no named donor;
  - many donors in Tower Hamlets pay little or no income tax, and Gift Aid requires tax paid ≥ the amount reclaimed (HMRC chapter 3);
  - madrasa fees and membership are excluded.
- Brick Lane's 9.5% might be the ceiling, not the norm.
- Community accountants could already offer this. If they don't, the margins may be poor.

**Counter-evidence searches I ran:**
1. "mosques missing out on Gift Aid… service 2025/2026": found **only Masjidbox's auto-claim** and mosque donation pages; no dedicated recovery service for mosques. That is a gap, and also a possible sign of weak demand.
2. "accountants East London mosque charity Gift Aid claims service": **NOT FOUND**. No accountant markets mosque Gift Aid. Two mosques list an accountant's email as their register contact (Poplar Central Mosque → rahmanandco.com; Masjid-E-Quba → alchemyassociates.co.uk), so accountants are the gatekeepers.
3. "HMRC Gift Aid fraud mosque/madrasa 2024–2026": no faith-specific recent case found. Older cases include an accountant jailed for 6 years for fake claims (Accountancy Age 2016, SEARCH-SUMMARY). **Implication:** our zero-false-claim checklist is part of the product.
4. The Brick Lane accounts themselves (E3) show that even a recognised Bangladeshi mosque's claim fell 84% in a year. A lapse market exists among recognised mosques too.

**Net:** the gap is real and public. The unknowns are willingness and the taxpayer share, which only replies and one real claim will reveal.

## (j) Intelligence verdict
The formula is close to right here. The proof is public and exact, the triggers are statutory dates, and the money comes from HMRC rather than the buyer's pocket. That is the strongest Pay logic in M8. Where the formula misleads: it scores Read on mechanism and ignores **willingness**. A mosque that never registered may have reasons a letter can't fix. The fee form is also a hidden halal plane: a % fee would look "standard" and quietly fail with Hanafi committees.

What I believe: this is M8's best road and worth the $0 week. The real buyer is probably the **examiner accountant**, not the mosque itself. Helper 1 found AM Accountancy Services signing 4 Bangladeshi mosques' accounts with ≈ £570k of donations and no Gift Aid between them. That one email is worth more than 20 mosque emails.
