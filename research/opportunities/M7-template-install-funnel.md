# M7 — Template/skill store → install upsell (the CEO's $29-asset route, tested)
Opus companion, 2026-10-29. Model: M7 Platform-Native Seller (hunting ground 2: template and skill stores).

**One line:** sell $29 automation templates and agent skills (n8n, Make, Claude/OpenClaw skills) on Gumroad, list free versions in the official n8n template library and on ClawHub, and convert buyers into $150–300 install jobs.

**Why test it:** the CEO already sells $29 products on Gumroad and builds OpenClaw skills, and models.md names "assets sell without hours" as M7's cap breaker. The question is whether buyers actually find and pay for this kind of asset.

**Short answer from the data: not as a lead route.** Paid automation assets are drowning in free ones, supply is accelerating, and sales go to creators who already have an audience. Keep one free asset as a credibility piece, not as a business.

## (h) Red team
**Strongest reason it fails:** distribution. In every store we measured, the products that sell belong to creators with audiences, and most of them are free lead magnets for courses and consulting. A two-person team without an audience is one listing among thousands, and supply doubled this year.
**Counter-evidence search I ran:** (1) Gumroad search sorted by most reviewed for "n8n", "make.com", "zapier", "claude skills", "openclaw", "clawhub", "mcp server", "claude code" (E3). Paid items with traction exist (e.g., "Claude Code Playbook" $147, 64 reviews), but all from audience-holders. (2) n8n library paid share by cohort (E3): falling. (3) OpenClaw service demand by month on Freelancer.com (E3): peaked April 2026. Nothing found that shows an unknown seller selling templates at volume.

**OpenClaw / ClawHub specifically (the CEO's ecosystem):**
- ClawHub hosts 67,000+ skills, free to list and install (SEARCH-SUMMARY, parallel.ai, 2026-08-18). No paid skill market exists on ClawHub, so money must come from installs or custom skills.
- Paid installs are real but the wave passed: Chinese on-site installs at 500 yuan per session (SEARCH-SUMMARY, Tencent Cloud techpedia); Western "done-for-you" vendors at $3,000–6,000 (VENDOR CLAIM). Freelancer.com OpenClaw posts fell from 22 (Apr 2026) to 3 (Sep 2026), with 29–220 bids each (E3).
- The security wave (ClawHavoc: 341 malicious skills, Koi Security, SEARCH-SUMMARY) is already served by vendors and built-in scanners (M4 summary, cited).
- **Verdict on OpenClaw:** no evidence that buyers now pay for skill installs or custom skills at a volume worth chasing. Don't build the business on it.
