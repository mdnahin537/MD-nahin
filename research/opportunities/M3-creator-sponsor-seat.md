## What it is
Media owners publicly post "commission-only sponsorship sales" seats. We apply, and if taken on we sell their inventory to sponsors (halal sponsors only). We earn 10–35% of each deal we close, plus renewals, and invoice our commission via Payoneer after the sponsor has paid the owner.

## (c) Filters
- **Work level:** medium-high. Sponsor research, pitch writing, negotiation and IO paperwork are not a 10-minute job.
- **Demand trend, dated:**
  - Posted commission seats in my opened sample run 1 (Nov 2025) → 1 (Jan 2026) → 3 (Jul 2026) → 1 (Aug 2026) → 2 open now (Oct 2026). This is E3 from Podnews, not exhaustive.
  - Podcast spend: +17.6% in 2025 (SEARCH-SUMMARY), +28% YoY in Q1 2026 (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
  - **Rising supply of automation for the same task:** Book a Sponsor, with 39,151 pitches in 30 days (VENDOR CLAIM, 2026-10-30), and beehiiv's network at ~$1M/month (SEARCH-SUMMARY).
- **AI-substitution risk (12–24 months): HIGH for discovery and pitching** (already productised: $19/month for 600 emails). **Low for relationships, negotiation and closing**, which is exactly the part posts demand experience for.
- **Team fit:** the CEO's spoken English and research suit the memo; US-hours calls and the closed-deal record do not fit. The partner's checklist strength suits IO and tracking admin.
- **Halal:**
  - Sell only halal sponsors.
  - Among Magellan's September 2026 top-15 US podcast spenders ($72.1M in total), **betting (DraftKings) is 7.7%** and **conventional insurance (Progressive, State Farm) is 10.2%**, so a strict screen removes ~18% of top-tier spend (my arithmetic on VERIFIED figures).
  - Long-tail shows sell mostly DTC/SaaS (ESTIMATE), so the screen removes less there. BNPL, crypto and alcohol sponsors are common in the mid-tier: screen each deal.

## (e) Money path and 3 scenarios
**Path:** one seat → first closed deal ($500–1,500) → 25–35% = $125–525 → 2–3 renewing sponsors on one or two seats → $1,000+/month (ESTIMATE).

**Formula:** seats reachable per year × reply × acceptance × deals per seat per year × deal size × commission.

| Scenario | Assumptions (all ESTIMATE except posted rates) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 8 unfiltered seats/yr × 10% reply × 30% accepted = 0.24 seats; 2 deals/yr × $500 × 25% | **≈ $60/yr** |
| Mid | 10 × 20% × 50% = 1 seat; 6 deals/yr × $1,200 × 30% | **≈ $2,160/yr (~$180/month)** |
| High | 15 × 30% × 60% = 2.7 seats; 10 deals/yr × $2,500 × 30% + 20% renewals | **≈ $20,000–24,000/yr (~$1,800/month)** |

Months retained: seats run while deals close. A seat with no deal in 90 days ends (ESTIMATE).

## (h) Red team
- **Strongest reason it fails:** owners hire commission reps for their **relationships**. Discovery and pitching, the part we could do, now costs about 3–10¢ an email with no commission (Book a Sponsor), converts at ~0.3% "yes" per cold pitch (VENDOR CLAIM), and the posts we can see mostly filter for US residence or years of closed deals. A zero-record team in Dhaka gets the seats nobody else wants: tiny shows (98 downloads in 28 days) whose 30% of nothing is nothing.
- **Counter-evidence searches run:**
  1. "reddit podcasting commission only sales rep… never closed": no first-hand threads returned. The results were only job posts, which themselves show the filters.
  2. "newsletter owner… sponsorship broker offers": Power Sponsorship (2019, VERIFIED page) warns that an unsolicited offer "promising revenue and/or specifying the retainer and commission, without ever having even spoken to you, is a scam". So an unsolicited commission pitch reads as a scam pattern to informed owners. That is why only posted seats are worth approaching.
  3. Sponsor-side reply evidence: Book a Sponsor's live 3.1 yes per 1,000 pitches.

## (i) vs Shothik (Read, Trust, Pay)
- **Read: better.** A posted application inbox beats Facebook message requests.
- **Trust: worse.** Shothik shows proof about the buyer's own listing. Here the buyer judges our sales record, which is zero.
- **Pay: same to worse.** Both are paid after an outcome via a Payoneer request. Here the money first has to pass from a third party (the sponsor) to the owner before we can collect, which adds a step and a delay.

## (j) Intelligence verdict
The formula flatters this: Read is a genuine 2 and the posted rates are real, so it looks like M3's "Read ✔". But the seat is a job interview, not a sale, and the interview question is "which brands have you closed?". With discovery and pitching productised at a few cents an email, what owners pay 20–35% for is a contact book. I would send **one** application with a memo to PodKeys before 4 Nov as a $0 probe, and not build a business on this route.
