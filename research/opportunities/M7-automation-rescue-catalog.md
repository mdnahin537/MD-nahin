# M7 — Automation Rescue: fixed-price "fix one broken Make/n8n/Zapier workflow" on Upwork Project Catalog
Opus companion, 2026-10-29. Model: M7 Platform-Native Seller (hunting ground 1: Upwork Catalog for tools; hunting ground 5: tool partner directories).

**One line:** a narrow, fixed-scope Catalog project ("I find and fix why your Make/n8n/Zapier workflow fails, in 48 hours, with a written cause and a re-test"), plus 1–3 proposals a month to posted "fix my workflow" jobs. It uses the CEO's real Make/n8n experience and the partner's checklist testing.

**Why this shape and not "automation builds":** builds are the killed "generic AI chatbot/automation agency". A fix has a trigger (something broke), a narrow scope, and a checkable result. That is the best version of the CEO's automation asset inside M7.

**Short answer from the data: crowded and getting worse per seller.** Keep a free Catalog listing as a passive door; don't make it the lead route.

## (e) Money path and estimate
**Path:** $40–80 Catalog fix → review → $150 multi-workflow fix → $150–300/month "on call" retainer for 2–3 clients who keep breaking things. Cap breaker would be a placed Dhaka helper under the partner's checklist (M2 Mode B), only after 10+ reviews.

Formula: proposals × win rate × price + Catalog orders × price, per month.
| Scenario | Assumptions (all ESTIMATE) | Month 3 | Month 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low (free Connects only) | 2 proposals/month × 5% win × $60; 0–1 Catalog order/quarter at $40 | ≈ $10 | ≈ $30 |
| Mid (+$15/month: Freelancer Plus or 100 Connects) | 10–12 proposals/month × 8% × $80 ≈ 1 win/month; 1 Catalog order/month at $60 after 3 reviews; 1 retainer at $150 by month 9 | ≈ $140 | ≈ $350 |
| High | 15% win after 5 reviews; 3 Catalog orders/month at $100; 3 retainers at $200 | ≈ $250 | ≈ $1,200 (near the hours ceiling) |
**What $10–15/month of Connects changes:** at $0.15 per Connect (models.md, SEARCH-SUMMARY), $15 buys 100 Connects; a third-party guide says Freelancer Plus at $14.99/month includes 100 Connects (SEARCH-SUMMARY). At an assumed 6–16 Connects per proposal (ESTIMATE; Upwork sets it per job), that is 6–16 more proposals a month, i.e. roughly **+0.5–1 win/month** at 5–8%. It pays for itself only if the win rate is ≥ 5% and the average job ≥ $60. Spend it only after the free test shows ≥ 1 reply in 5 proposals.

## (h) Red team
**Strongest reason it fails:** the buyer side is shrinking and the seller side is exploding. On the one public marketplace we can measure, n8n posts fell about 4.6× in 11 months while bids per post rose about 3.6×; on Upwork the posted fix budgets are $5–30, i.e. buyers expect AI-speed prices. A zero-review Dhaka team competes with hundreds of other zero-review sellers, many from Bangladesh, Pakistan and India.
**Counter-evidence search I ran:** "Upwork 2026 report demand AI agent / workflow automation / n8n growth" → found only the Q2 2026 earnings summary (AI-related GSV up, simple work automated faster) and vendor blogs ("is n8n dead in 2026", growwstacks) — nothing showing rising per-seller demand for automation fixes. Upwork search for Oct 2026 n8n fix posts → $5–30 budgets. n8n Expert Partner criteria → region-locked. Net: no evidence lifts Reply.

## (j) Intelligence verdict
The formula flatters platform ideas: "Read, Trust and Pay are carried by the platform" is true only for sellers who already have reviews. For a new seller, Read-noticed and Trust-competence are 1s, Reply is a lottery against 100+ bids, and the dated trend is down while AI eats the simple fixes. **I would not spend more than the free Connects here.** Keep the Catalog listing as a $0 passive door that the CEO's real experience can fill when a buyer finds it, and point the scarce evenings at the red-team ladder (rank 1).
