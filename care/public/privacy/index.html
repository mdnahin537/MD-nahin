Privacy & terms

Plain language. No surprises.

What this is

This board is where RealmWright players report bugs and request features. Reading it needs no account. Posting a report, agreeing, disagreeing, or commenting needs a Google sign-in — so each contribution has a name we can follow up with.

What we store

  • Your Google display name and profile photo — shown publicly on your reports and comments.
  • Your email address — stored so the owner can follow up with you about a tricky report. It is never shown publicly.
  • Your reports, votes, and comments — these are public.
  • Coarse technical context you choose to attach — app version, mode, theme, and your browser/OS family. You see exactly what will be attached before you send, and you can remove any of it. We never receive your world data, your files, or any API keys.

What is public

Your reports, ideas, comments, votes, display name, and photo are visible to anyone. Your email is not. The board doubles as our public roadmap, so it may be linked from our store pages.

Deleting your data

Email us and we will remove your account. Your past reports and comments stay on the board but are renamed to “a GM”, so the record of what was reported isn’t lost.

Terms, briefly

Be kind and keep it on-topic. Spam, abuse, and off-topic content may be hidden. One account per person; one vote per report. That’s it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact the owner.

This page is set at deploy time; the contact address is configured by the site owner.
