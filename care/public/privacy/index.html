Privacy & terms

Plain language. No surprises.

What this is

This board is where RealmWright players report bugs and request features. Reading it needs no account. Posting a report, agreeing, disagreeing, or commenting creates a private local Care identity in your browser so each contribution has a stable name. There is no Google login and no password.

What we store

  • Your Care identity name — a generic local name such as “A GM”, shown publicly on your reports and comments.
  • No email address or Google profile — this version does not collect either.
  • Your reports, votes, and comments — these are public.
  • Coarse technical context you choose to attach — app version, mode, theme, and your browser/OS family. You see exactly what will be attached before you send, and you can remove any of it. We never receive your world data, your files, or any API keys.

What is public

Your reports, ideas, comments, votes, and Care identity name are visible to anyone. No email or Google profile is collected by this version. The board doubles as our public roadmap, so it may be linked from our store pages.

Deleting your data

Use the contact address below to request deletion. Your past reports and comments stay on the board but are detached from the deleted identity and shown as “a GM”, so the record of what was reported isn’t lost.

Terms, briefly

Be kind and keep it on-topic. Spam, abuse, and off-topic content may be hidden. One account per person; one vote per report. That’s it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact the owner.
