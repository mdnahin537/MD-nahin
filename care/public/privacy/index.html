Privacy & terms

Plain language. No surprises.

What this is

This board is where RealmWright players report bugs and request features. Reading it needs no account. Posting a report, agreeing, disagreeing, or commenting creates a private local Care identity in your browser so each contribution has a stable name. There is no Google login and no password.

What we store

  • Your Care identity name — a generic local name such as “A GM”, shown publicly on your reports and comments.
  • No email address or Google profile — this version does not collect either.
  • Your reports, ideas, and comments — these are public unless held for review. Public vote totals show agreement; your device’s individual vote is shown to you.
  • Coarse technical context you choose to attach — app version, data schema version, build, mode, theme, AI setting, browser/OS family, screen category, and language. You see exactly what will be attached before you send, and you can remove any of it. Do not paste world data, private customer details, license keys, or API keys into a report or comment.

What is public

Your published reports, ideas, comments, Care identity name, and vote totals are visible to anyone. Submissions held for review stay out of the public board. No email or Google profile is collected by this version. The board doubles as our public roadmap, so it may be linked from our store pages.

Saved drafts and email copies

An unfinished report or comment stays in this browser tab for up to 24 hours. Reloading the tab keeps it. Sending or discarding it clears the draft; signing out clears drafts in this tab. Closing the tab may remove it. Recovery codes are never stored with drafts.

Your browser can also remember whether you paused decorative motion. This preference stays on your device.

Customer Care stores each submission. A complete additional copy of reports, comments, and follow-up answers is sent to the owner through Brevo and received in the owner’s Gmail mailbox. A saved report remains available even when email delivery is delayed. Public confirmation means the report was saved; it does not confirm email receipt.

Keeping your identity

Your Care identity belongs to this browser profile. Changing browsers, clearing cookies, or moving to another device requires the one-time recovery code you saved. Keep it private and offline. After recovery, generate and save a new code.

Deleting your data

Use the contact address below to request deletion. The owner can discuss removing identifying details while preserving the record of reported problems. There is no automatic deletion button.

Terms, briefly

Be kind and keep it on-topic. Spam, abuse, and off-topic content may be hidden. One account per person; one vote per report. That’s it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact the owner.

This page is set at deploy time; the contact address is configured by the site owner.
